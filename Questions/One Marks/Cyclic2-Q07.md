7. Assertion(A): Ortho-nitrophenol is more steam volatile than para-nitrophenol.
Reason(R): Ortho-nitrophenol possesses intramolecular hydrogen bonding, whereas para-nitrophenol shows intermolecular hydrogen bonding.

- A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
- B) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
- C) (A) is correct but (R) is wrong
- D) (A) is wrong but (R) is correct

## Answer

**A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)**
Intramolecular hydrogen bonding in ortho-nitrophenol prevents association between molecules making it steam volatile, whereas intermolecular hydrogen bonding in para-nitrophenol leads to strong molecular association and higher boiling point.
