Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

4. Which of the following compounds is formed when benzyl alcohol is oxidised with KMnO₄?

- A) CO₂ and H₂O
- B) Benzoic acid
- C) Benzaldehyde
- D) Benzophenone

## Answer

**B) Benzoic acid**
Acidified or alkaline KMnO₄ is a strong oxidizing agent that oxidizes primary aromatic alcohols like benzyl alcohol (C₆H₅CH₂OH) completely to benzoic acid (C₆H₅COOH).
