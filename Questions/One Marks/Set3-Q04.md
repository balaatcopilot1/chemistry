Chapter: Coordination Compounds

4. According to Werner’s theory, the primary valencies of the central metal atom :

- A) are satisfied by neutral molecules or negative ions.
- B) are equal to its coordination number.
- C) are satisfied by negative ions.
- D) are non-ionisable.

## Answer

**C) are satisfied by negative ions.**
According to Werner's theory, primary valencies correspond to the oxidation state of the central metal atom, are ionisable, and are satisfied exclusively by negative ions.
