Chapter: Amines

4. The graphs below show the solubility of a primary, a secondary and a tertiary aliphatic amine I, J, and K in water, at the same temperature. The number of carbon atoms in each of the compounds is three. Amine I is the tertiary amine, amine J is the primary amine, and amine K is the secondary amine.
Which of the graphs identifies the three amines correctly?

- A) P
- B) Q
- C) R
- D) S

## Answer

**C) R**
Primary amines (J) have two hydrogen atoms on nitrogen available for hydrogen bonding with water, secondary amines (K) have one, and tertiary amines (I) have none, making the solubility order J > K > I.
