2. Calculate molarity of nitrate ions in the mixture of solution produced by mixing 2 litre 3M AgNO₃ solution with 3 litre 1 M BaCl₂ solution?

- A) 1.2 M
- B) 1.8 M
- C) 0.5 M
- D) 0.4 M

## Answer

**A) 1.2 M**
Moles of NO₃⁻ from AgNO₃ = 2 L × 3 mol L⁻¹ = 6 mol. Total volume of mixture = 2 L + 3 L = 5 L. Molarity of NO₃⁻ = 6 mol / 5 L = 1.2 M.
