1. Which has highest freezing point:

- A) 1 M Glucose
- B) 1 M NaCl
- C) 1 M BaCl₂
- D) 1 M AlF₃

## Answer

**A) 1 M Glucose**
Glucose is a non-electrolyte (i = 1), giving the smallest van 't Hoff factor and least depression in freezing point (ΔTf = i Kf m), so its freezing point remains the highest among the given solutions.
