Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

8. For the non-stoichiometric reaction 2A + B → C + D, the following kinetic data were obtained in three separate experiments, all at 298 K.
[A] = 0.1 M, [B] = 0.1 M, Initial rate = 1.2 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹
[A] = 0.1 M, [B] = 0.2 M, Initial rate = 1.2 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹
[A] = 0.2 M, [B] = 0.1 M, Initial rate = 2.4 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹
The rate law for the formation of C is:

- A) d[C]/dt = k [A]² [B]
- B) d[C]/dt = k [A] [B]²
- C) d[C]/dt = k [A] [B]
- D) d[C]/dt = k [A]

## Answer

**D) d[C]/dt = k [A]**
Doubling [B] does not alter the reaction rate (zero-order w.r.t B), whereas doubling [A] doubles the rate (first-order w.r.t A).
