Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

2. Lucas reagent produces cloudiness immediately with :

- A) 2-methyl-1-propanol
- B) Butan-2-ol
- C) 2-methyl-2-propanol
- D) Butan-1-ol

## Answer

**C) 2-methyl-2-propanol**
Tertiary alcohols like 2-methyl-2-propanol react immediately with Lucas reagent (conc. HCl + anhydrous ZnCl₂) to form an insoluble alkyl chloride that produces instant turbidity.

*Source: NCERT, Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers*
