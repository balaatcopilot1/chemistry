Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

1. Which of the following will be the least reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction ?

- A) Benzyl chloride
- B) 4-Chlorotoluene
- C) CH₃–Cl
- D) Chlorocyclohexane

![Diagram](Questions/images/Set3-Q01.png)
The diagram shows the chemical structures of benzyl chloride, 4-chlorotoluene, methyl chloride, and chlorocyclohexane.

## Answer

**B) 4-Chlorotoluene**
In 4-chlorotoluene, the C–Cl bond acquires partial double bond character due to resonance delocalization of chlorine lone pairs into the benzene ring, making it the least reactive towards nucleophilic substitution.
