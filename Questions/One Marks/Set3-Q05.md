Chapter: Amines

5. Which of the following reagent is used to distinguish between (C₂H₅)₂NH and (C₂H₅)₃N ?

- A) CHCl₃ + KOH
- B) C₆H₅SO₂Cl
- C) Conc. HCl + ZnCl₂
- D) NaOH + I₂

## Answer

**B) C₆H₅SO₂Cl**
Benzenesulfonyl chloride (Hinsberg reagent, C₆H₅SO₂Cl) reacts with secondary amine (C₂H₅)₂NH to form an alkali-insoluble sulfonamide, whereas tertiary amine (C₂H₅)₃N does not react.
