Chapter: Electrochemistry

3. ΔG and E°cell for a spontaneous reaction will be:

- A) positive, negative
- B) negative, negative
- C) negative, positive
- D) positive, positive

## Answer

**C) negative, positive**
For a spontaneous process, the change in Gibbs free energy (ΔG) must be negative, which corresponds to a positive standard cell potential (E°cell) via ΔG° = -nFE°cell.

*Source: NCERT, Electrochemistry*
