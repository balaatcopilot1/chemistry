Chapter: Coordination Compounds

3. The oxidation number of Co in [Co(en)₃]₂(SO₄)₃ is :

- A) +3
- B) +2
- C) +4
- D) +6

## Answer

**A) +3**
Ethane-1,2-diamine (en) is a neutral ligand with zero charge, and sulfate (SO₄²⁻) has a charge of -2; for 2 Co atoms, 2x + 3(-2) = 0 ⇒ x = +3.

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
