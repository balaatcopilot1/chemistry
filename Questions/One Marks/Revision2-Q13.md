13. Assertion (A): Ebullioscopic constant is constant for a particular solvent at a particular temperature and does not depend upon the solute.
Reason (R): Elevation in boiling point depends upon the mass of the solute dissolved in the definite mass of a particular solvent and is independent of the nature of the solute and solvent.

- A) Assertion and reason are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is a correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement.

## Answer

**B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.**
Kb is a characteristic thermodynamic constant of the solvent [Kb = M · R · (Tb)² / (1000 · ΔvapH)], while elevation in boiling point is a colligative property dependent solely on the number of solute particles.
