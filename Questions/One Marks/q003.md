In the reaction N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃, increasing the pressure shifts the equilibrium towards:

- A) Reactants
- B) Products
- C) No shift
- D) Cannot be predicted

## Answer

**B) Products.** The forward reaction reduces the number of gas moles (4 → 2), so higher pressure favours NH₃ (Le Chatelier’s principle).
