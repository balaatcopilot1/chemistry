7. The boiling point of one molal NaCl solution, assuming NaCl to be completely dissociated in water is : (Kb for water = 0.52 K kg mol⁻¹)

- A) 100.52 ºC
- B) 101.04 ºC
- C) 100.04 ºC
- D) 101.52 ºC

## Answer

**B) 101.04 ºC**
For complete dissociation of NaCl into Na⁺ and Cl⁻, i = 2. Elevation in boiling point ΔTb = i × Kb × m = 2 × 0.52 × 1 = 1.04 ºC, giving a boiling point of 100 ºC + 1.04 ºC = 101.04 ºC.
