Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

7. In aqueous solution, Cr₂O⇇²⁻ ion converts to which of the following in alkaline medium ?

- A) Cr³⁺
- B) CrO₄²⁻
- C) CrO
- D) CrO₃

## Answer

**B) CrO₄²⁻**
In alkaline medium (pH > 7), orange dichromate ion (Cr₂O⇇²⁻) converts into yellow chromate ion (CrO₄²⁻) according to the equilibrium: Cr₂O⇇²⁻ + 2 OH⁻ ⇌ 2 CrO₄²⁻ + H₂O.

*Source: NCERT, The d- and f-Block Elements*
