Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

7. A flask contains a mixture of compounds A and B. Both compounds decompose by first-order kinetics. The half-lives for A and B are 300 s and 180 s, respectively. If the concentrations of A and B are equal initially, the time required for the concentration of A to be four times that of B (in s) is: (Use ln 2 = 0.693)

- A) 900
- B) 300
- C) 120
- D) 180

## Answer

**A) 900**
At t = 900 s, compound A undergoes 3 half-lives ([A] = [A]₀ / 8) and compound B undergoes 5 half-lives ([B] = [B]₀ / 32), giving [A] / [B] = 32 / 8 = 4.
