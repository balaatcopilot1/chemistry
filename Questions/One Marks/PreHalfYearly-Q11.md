Chapter: Amines

11. Reaction of a primary amine with an excess of alkyl halide (CH₃I) in the presence of a base ultimately yields a:

- A) Secondary amine
- B) Tertiary amine
- C) Quaternary ammonium salt
- D) Amide

## Answer

**C) Quaternary ammonium salt**
Primary amines undergo successive nucleophilic substitution (exhaustive methylation) with excess alkyl halide until all nitrogen hydrogens are replaced, forming a quaternary ammonium salt R–N⁺(CH₃)₃ I⁻.
