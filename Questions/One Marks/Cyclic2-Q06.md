Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

6. The major product obtained when phenol reacts with bromine water is:

- A) 2-Bromophenol
- B) 4-Bromophenol
- C) 2,4,6-Tribromophenol
- D) 3-Bromophenol

## Answer

**C) 2,4,6-Tribromophenol**
In aqueous medium, phenol ionizes to phenoxide ion, highly activating the aromatic ring towards electrophilic substitution at all ortho and para positions simultaneously to yield a white precipitate of 2,4,6-tribromophenol.

*Source: NCERT, Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers*
