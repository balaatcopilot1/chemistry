Chapter: Amines

6. Among the following, which is the strongest base ?

- A) 4-Nitroaniline
- B) 4-Methylaniline
- C) Benzylamine
- D) Aniline

![Diagram](Questions/images/Set3-Q06.png)
The diagram shows the chemical structures of 4-nitroaniline, 4-methylaniline, benzylamine, and aniline.

## Answer

**C) Benzylamine**
In benzylamine (C₆H₅CH₂NH₂), the nitrogen lone pair is attached to an sp³ carbon and is not delocalized into the aromatic ring, making it a stronger base than arylamines where lone pairs participate in resonance.
