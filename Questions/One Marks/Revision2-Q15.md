Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

15. Assertion (A): Rate constant of a zero order reaction has the same unit as rate of a reaction.
Reason (R): Rate of a zero order reaction depends on concentration.

- A) Assertion and reason are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is a correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement.

## Answer

**C) Assertion is a correct statement but reason is wrong statement.**
For a zero-order reaction, Rate = k [A]⁰ = k, so both rate and rate constant share units of mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹; however, the rate of a zero-order reaction is independent of reactant concentration.
