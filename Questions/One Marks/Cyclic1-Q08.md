Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

8. Assertion (A): Haloalkanes can be prepared by the reaction of alcohols with halogen acids.
Reason (R): The reaction always proceeds via the formation of a carbocation intermediate, regardless of the type of alcohol.

- A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
- B) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
- C) (A) is correct but (R) is wrong
- D) (A) is wrong but (R) is correct

## Answer

**C) (A) is correct but (R) is wrong**
Primary alcohols react with halogen acids via a concerted SN2 mechanism without forming carbocation intermediates.
