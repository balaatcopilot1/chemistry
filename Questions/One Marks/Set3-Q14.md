Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

14. Assertion (A) : Phenol is less acidic than 4-methylphenol.
Reason (R) : The presence of an electron releasing group in phenol makes it less acidic.

- A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).
- B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).
- C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.
- D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.

## Answer

**D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.**
Phenol is more acidic (pKa ~ 10) than 4-methylphenol (pKa ~ 10.2) because the electron-releasing methyl group (+I / hyperconjugation) destabilizes the phenoxide ion and reduces acidity.

*Source: NCERT, Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers*
