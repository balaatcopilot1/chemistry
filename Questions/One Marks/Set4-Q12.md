Chapter: Amines

12. In the reaction of NaOBr with amide, the carbonyl carbon is lost as :

- A) HCO₃⁻
- B) CO₃²⁻
- C) CO₂
- D) CO

## Answer

**B) CO₃²⁻**
In the Hofmann bromamide degradation reaction (RCONH₂ + NaOBr + 2 NaOH → RNH₂ + Na₂CO₃ + NaBr + H₂O), the carbonyl carbon is eliminated as sodium carbonate (CO₃²⁻).
