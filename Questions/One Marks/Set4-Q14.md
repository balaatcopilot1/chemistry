Chapter: Amines

14. Assertion (A) : Diazonium salts of aromatic amines are more stable than those of aliphatic amines.
Reason (R) : Diazonium salts of aliphatic amines undergo resonance.

- A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).
- B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).
- C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.
- D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.

## Answer

**C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.**
Arenediazonium salts are stabilized by resonance delocalization of the positive charge over the benzene ring, whereas aliphatic diazonium salts cannot undergo resonance and decompose rapidly even at low temperatures.

*Source: NCERT, Amines*
