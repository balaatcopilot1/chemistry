Chapter: Coordination Compounds

3. Which of the following d-orbitals experience more repulsion in the crystal field splitting of octahedral complex ?

- A) dxy , dyz , dxz
- B) dx² – y², dz²
- C) dxy , dx² – y²
- D) dxz , dz²

## Answer

**B) dx² – y², dz²**
In an octahedral crystal field, ligands approach along the x, y, and z axes, causing the eg orbitals (dx²–y² and dz²) pointing directly at the ligands to experience stronger repulsion and rise in energy.

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
