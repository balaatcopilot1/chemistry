13. Assertion : When methyl alcohol is added to water, boiling point of water increases.
Reason : When a volatile solute is added to a volatile solvent, elevation in boiling point is observed.

- A) Assertion and reason both are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement
- E) Assertion and Reason both are wrong statements

## Answer

**E) Assertion and Reason both are wrong statements**
Methyl alcohol is more volatile than water; adding a volatile solute increases total vapour pressure and lowers the boiling point of the mixture, so no elevation in boiling point occurs.
