Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

4. Which of the following is not chiral?

- A) 2-Hydroxypropanoic acid
- B) 2-Butanol
- C) 2,3-Dibromobutane
- D) 3-Bromopentane

## Answer

**D) 3-Bromopentane**
3-Bromopentane has a central carbon bonded to two identical ethyl groups (–CH₂–CH₃), making it symmetrical and achiral.
