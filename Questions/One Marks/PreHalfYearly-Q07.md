Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

7. Which of the following is most reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction?

- A) C₆H₅Cl
- B) CH₂=CHCl
- C) ClCH₂CH=CH₂
- D) CH₃CH=CHCl

## Answer

**C) ClCH₂CH=CH₂**
Allyl chloride (ClCH₂CH=CH₂) undergoes rapid nucleophilic substitution because loss of chloride ion yields an allyl carbocation stabilized by resonance delocalization.
