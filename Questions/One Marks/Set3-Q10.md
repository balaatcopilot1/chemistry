Chapter: Electrochemistry

10. On electrolysis of very dilute aqueous solution of NaCl using platinum electrodes :

- A) H₂ gas is evolved at anode.
- B) Na is produced at cathode.
- C) O₂ gas is evolved at anode.
- D) H₂ gas is evolved at cathode.

## Answer

**C) O₂ gas is evolved at anode.**
In a very dilute NaCl solution, oxidation of water (2 H₂O → O₂ + 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻) is thermodynamically favored at the anode over chloride oxidation, releasing O₂ gas at the anode (and H₂ gas at the cathode).
