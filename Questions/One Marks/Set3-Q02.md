Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

2. The intermediate formed during the slowest step involved in the dehydration of alcohol is :

- A) protonated alcohol
- B) carbanion
- C) free radical
- D) carbocation

## Answer

**D) carbocation**
Dehydration of alcohols proceeds via a mechanism where the slow, rate-determining step is the loss of a water molecule from the protonated alcohol to form a carbocation intermediate.
