Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

2. The correct order of number of unpaired electrons is

- A) Cu²⁺ > Ni²⁺ > Cr³⁺ > Fe³⁺
- B) Ni²⁺ > Cu²⁺ > Fe³⁺ > Cr³⁺
- C) Fe³⁺ > Cr³⁺ > Ni²⁺ > Cu²⁺
- D) Cr³⁺ > Fe³⁺ > Ni²⁺ > Cu²⁺

## Answer

**C) Fe³⁺ > Cr³⁺ > Ni²⁺ > Cu²⁺**
Fe³⁺ (3d⁵) has 5 unpaired electrons, Cr³⁺ (3d³) has 3, Ni²⁺ (3d⁸) has 2, and Cu²⁺ (3d⁹) has 1 unpaired electron.
