2. An aqueous solution of a non-volatile solute freezes at -0.186 °C. Given that the molal depression constant (Kf) for water is 1.86 K kg mol⁻¹ and the molal elevation constant (Kb) is 0.512 K kg mol⁻¹, what is the boiling point of this solution at 1 atm?

- A) 100.186 °C
- B) 99.949 °C
- C) 100.512 °C
- D) 100.0512 °C

## Answer

**D) 100.0512 °C**
Molality m = ΔTf / Kf = 0.186 / 1.86 = 0.1 mol kg⁻¹; Elevation in boiling point ΔTb = Kb · m = 0.512 × 0.1 = 0.0512 °C; Boiling point = 100.0000 °C + 0.0512 °C = 100.0512 °C.
