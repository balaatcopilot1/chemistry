9. Which plot of ln k vs 1/T is consistent with Arrhenius equation?

- A) (A)
- B) (B)
- C) (C)
- D) (D)

## Answer

**D) (D)**
According to the Arrhenius equation ln k = ln A - Eₐ / (RT), plotting ln k versus 1/T yields a straight line with a negative slope equal to -Eₐ / R.
