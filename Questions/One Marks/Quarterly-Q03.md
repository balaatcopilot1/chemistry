3. If limiting molar conductivity of Ca²⁺ and Cl⁻ are 119.0 and 76.3 S cm² mol⁻¹, then the value of limiting molar conductivity of CaCl₂ will be

- A) 195.3 S cm² mol⁻¹
- B) 271.6 S cm² mol⁻¹
- C) 43.3 S cm² mol⁻¹
- D) 314.3 S cm² mol⁻¹

## Answer

**B) 271.6 S cm² mol⁻¹**
According to Kohlrausch's law, Λm°(CaCl₂) = λm°(Ca²⁺) + 2 × λm°(Cl⁻) = 119.0 + 2(76.3) = 119.0 + 152.6 = 271.6 S cm² mol⁻¹.
