Chapter: Biomolecules

11. Glucose reacts with acetic anhydride to form:

- A) penta-acetate
- B) hexa-acetate
- C) tetra-acetate
- D) monoacetate

## Answer

**A) penta-acetate**
Acetylation of glucose with acetic anhydride yields glucose pentaacetate, confirming the presence of five hydroxyl (–OH) groups.
