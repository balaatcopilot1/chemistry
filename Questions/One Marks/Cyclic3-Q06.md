Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

6. Acetone combines with ethylene glycol in dry HCl gas to generate

- A) cyclic ketals
- B) hemiacetals
- C) cyclic acetals
- D) acetals

## Answer

**A) cyclic ketals**
Ketones like acetone react with vicinal diols such as ethylene glycol in the presence of dry HCl gas to yield cyclic products known as cyclic ketals (ethylene glycol ketals).
