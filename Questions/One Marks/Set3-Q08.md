8. The boiling point of an azeotropic mixture of water and ethanol is less than that of pure water and ethanol. The mixture shows :

- A) positive deviation from Raoult’s Law.
- B) negative deviation from Raoult’s Law.
- C) no deviation from Raoult’s Law.
- D) that the solution is an ideal solution.

## Answer

**A) positive deviation from Raoult’s Law.**
Solutions showing large positive deviation from Raoult's Law have higher total vapour pressure than pure components and form minimum boiling azeotropes whose boiling point is lower than either component.
