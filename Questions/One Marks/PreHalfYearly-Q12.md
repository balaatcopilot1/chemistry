12. In the reaction A + 3B → 2C, the rate of formation of C is:

- A) the same as rate of consumption of A
- B) the same as rate of consumption of B
- C) twice the rate as consumption of A
- D) 3/2 times the rate of consumption of B

## Answer

**C) twice the rate as consumption of A**
From stoichiometry, Rate = -d[A]/dt = -1/3 d[B]/dt = +1/2 d[C]/dt, which gives d[C]/dt = 2 × (-d[A]/dt).
