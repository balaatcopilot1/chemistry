Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

10. Mark the correct order of decreasing acid strength of the following
I) P-Cyano phenol  II) P-nitro phenol  III) P-Chloro phenol  IV) P-methoxy phenol  V) Phenol

- A) I > II > III > IV > V
- B) I > III > II > IV > V
- C) II > I > III > V > IV
- D) IV > II > III > V

## Answer

**C) II > I > III > V > IV**
Strong electron-withdrawing groups (–NO₂ > –CN > –Cl) increase phenol acidity by stabilizing the phenoxide ion, while electron-donating groups (–OCH₃) decrease acidity relative to unsubstituted phenol.

*Source: NCERT, Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers*
