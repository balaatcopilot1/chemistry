Chapter: Coordination Compounds

6. Which of the following has highest molar conductivity?

- A) potassium hexacyanoferrate(II)
- B) hexaaquachromium(III) chloride
- C) tetraamminedichloridocobalt(III) chloride
- D) diamminedichloridoplatinum(II)

## Answer

**A) potassium hexacyanoferrate(II)**
Potassium hexacyanoferrate(II), K₄[Fe(CN)₆], dissociates in water to give 5 ions (4 K⁺ + [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻), producing the highest number of ions and greatest molar conductivity.

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
