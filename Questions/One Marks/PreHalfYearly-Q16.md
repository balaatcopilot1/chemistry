16. Assertion (A): When a non-volatile solute is added to a volatile solvent, its freezing point decreases.
Reason (R): Upon addition of a non-volatile solute to a volatile solvent, the vapour pressure decreases.

- A) Assertion and reason are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is a correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement.

## Answer

**A) Assertion and reason are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.**
Addition of a non-volatile solute lowers the vapour pressure of the solvent, causing the solution vapour pressure curve to intersect the solid solvent curve at a lower temperature, resulting in freezing point depression.
