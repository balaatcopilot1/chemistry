Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

1. Oxidation of chloroform by air in the presence of sunlight produces a poisonous gas known as :

- A) Mustard gas
- B) Tear gas
- C) Chlorine gas
- D) Phosgene gas

## Answer

**D) Phosgene gas**
Chloroform (CHCl₃) is slowly oxidized by air in the presence of light to form carbonyl chloride (COCl₂), commonly known as phosgene gas.

*Source: NCERT, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*
