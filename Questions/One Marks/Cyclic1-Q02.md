Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

2. Alkyl halides undergo a specific type of chemical reaction in order to be converted into alcohols. Which of the following reactions is responsible for this transformation?

- A) Addition reaction
- B) Substitution reaction
- C) Elimination reaction
- D) Dehydrohalogenation reaction

## Answer

**B) Substitution reaction**
Alkyl halides undergo nucleophilic substitution reactions when treated with aqueous KOH, replacing the halogen atom with a hydroxyl group to yield alcohols.
