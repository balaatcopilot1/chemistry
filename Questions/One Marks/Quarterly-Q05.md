Chapter: Coordination Compounds

5. The complex ions [Co(NH₃)₅(NO₂)]²⁺ and [Co(NH₃)₅(ONO)]²⁺ are called

- A) Ionization isomers
- B) Linkage isomers
- C) Co-ordination isomers
- D) Geometrical isomers

## Answer

**B) Linkage isomers**
The ambidentate ligand nitrite can bind to the central Cobalt metal through either nitrogen (–NO₂) or oxygen (–ONO), giving linkage isomerism.

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
