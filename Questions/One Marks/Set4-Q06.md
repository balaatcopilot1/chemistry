Chapter: Amines

6. The correct order of decreasing basicities of C₂H₅NH₂, (C₂H₅)₂NH and (C₂H₅)₃N in aqueous solution is :

- A) (C₂H₅)₃N > (C₂H₅)₂NH > C₂H₅NH₂
- B) (C₂H₅)₂NH > C₂H₅NH₂ > (C₂H₅)₃N
- C) C₂H₅NH₂ > (C₂H₅)₂NH > (C₂H₅)₃N
- D) (C₂H₅)₂NH > (C₂H₅)₃N > C₂H₅NH₂

## Answer

**D) (C₂H₅)₂NH > (C₂H₅)₃N > C₂H₅NH₂**
In aqueous solution, the basic strength of ethyl-substituted amines is determined by the combined inductive effect, steric hindrance, and cation hydration, following the order 2° > 3° > 1°.

*Source: NCERT, Amines*
