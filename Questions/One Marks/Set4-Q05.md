Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

5. Manganate ion is paramagnetic due to the presence of :

- A) one unpaired electron
- B) two unpaired electrons
- C) three unpaired electrons
- D) fully paired electrons

## Answer

**A) one unpaired electron**
In manganate ion (MnO₄²⁻), manganese is in the +6 oxidation state with a 3d¹ electronic configuration, giving one unpaired electron that imparts paramagnetism.
