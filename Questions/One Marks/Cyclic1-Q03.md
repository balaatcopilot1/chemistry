3. A car radiator contains a 35% (v/v) ethylene glycol solution used as an antifreeze. What does this concentration indicate?

- A) 35 g of ethylene glycol in 100 mL of water
- B) 35 mL of ethylene glycol in 100 mL of solution
- C) 35 mL of ethylene glycol in 100 g of solution
- D) 35 g of ethylene glycol in 100 g of solution

## Answer

**B) 35 mL of ethylene glycol in 100 mL of solution**
Volume percentage (v/v) represents the volume of solute in mL present in 100 mL of total solution.
