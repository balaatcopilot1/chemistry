Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

10. The rate of a first order reaction is 5.6 × 10⁻⁴ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹, when the concentration of reactant is 0.2 mol L⁻¹. The rate constant ‘k’ is :

- A) 5.6 × 10⁻³ s⁻¹
- B) 2.8 × 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹
- C) 2.8 × 10⁻⁵ s⁻¹
- D) 2.8 × 10⁻³ s⁻¹

## Answer

**D) 2.8 × 10⁻³ s⁻¹**
For a first-order reaction, Rate = k [A] ⇒ k = Rate / [A] = (5.6 × 10⁻⁴ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹) / (0.2 mol L⁻¹) = 2.8 × 10⁻³ s⁻¹.
