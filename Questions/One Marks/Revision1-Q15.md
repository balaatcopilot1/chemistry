Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

15. Assertion: Separation of Zr and Hf is difficult
Reason: Because Zr and Hf lie in the same group of the periodic table

- A) Assertion and reason both are correct statements and reason is correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason both are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement

## Answer

**C) Assertion is correct statement but reason is wrong statement.**
Separation of Zr and Hf is difficult because they have nearly identical atomic radii (160 pm and 159 pm) due to Lanthanoid Contraction, not simply because they belong to the same group.
