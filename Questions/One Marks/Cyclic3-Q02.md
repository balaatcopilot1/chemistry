Chapter: Electrochemistry

2. For a cell reaction involving a two-electron change, the standard e.m.f of the cell is found to be 0.295 V at 25°C. The equilibrium constant of the reaction at 25°C will be

- A) 1 × 10⁻¹⁰
- B) 29.5 × 10⁻²
- C) 10
- D) 1 × 10¹⁰

## Answer

**D) 1 × 10¹⁰**
Using the Nernst equation at equilibrium: E°cell = (0.0591 / n) log K. Substituting E°cell = 0.295 V and n = 2 gives log K = (0.295 × 2) / 0.0591 = 10, so K = 10¹⁰.
