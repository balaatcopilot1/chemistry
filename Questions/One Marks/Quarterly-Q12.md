Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

12. Which of the following compounds helps in reducing esters to aldehydes?

- A) BINAL-H
- B) DIBAL-H
- C) DIPT
- D) TBAF

## Answer

**B) DIBAL-H**
Diisobutylaluminium hydride (DIBAL-H) selectively reduces esters to aldehydes at low temperatures (-78°C) without further reduction to alcohols.
