Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

5. The correct order of acid strength of the following carboxylic acids is:
I: H–COOH
II: CH₂=CH–COOH
III: p-MeO–C₆H₄–COOH
IV: p-H₃C–C₆H₄–COOH

- A) III > II > I > IV
- B) I > III > II > IV
- C) I > II > IV > III
- D) II > I > IV > III

## Answer

**C) I > II > IV > III**
Formic acid (I) is the strongest acid because it lacks electron-donating alkyl groups. Acrylic acid (II) is next. Electron-donating groups decrease acidity, so p-methyl (IV, +I effect) is stronger than p-methoxy (III, strong +R effect).

*Source: NCERT, Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids*
