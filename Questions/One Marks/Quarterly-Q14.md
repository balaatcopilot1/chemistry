Chapter: Coordination Compounds

14. Assertion : [Ti(H₂O)₆]³⁺ is coloured while [Sc(H₂O)₆]³⁺ is colourless.
Reason : d-d transition is not possible in [Sc(H₂O)₆]³⁺.

- A) Assertion and reason both are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement

## Answer

**A) Assertion and reason both are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.**
Sc³⁺ has a 3d⁰ electronic configuration with no d-electrons available for d-d transitions, making its complex colourless, whereas Ti³⁺ has a 3d¹ configuration that undergoes d-d absorption in the visible spectrum.

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
