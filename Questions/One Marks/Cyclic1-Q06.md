Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

6. What is the IUPAC name for the compound (CH₃)₃CCH₂Cl?

- A) 1-Chloro-2,2-dimethylpropane
- B) 1-Chloro-2,2,2-trimethylethane
- C) 2-Chloromethyl-2-methylpropane
- D) 2,2-Dimethyl-1-Bromopropane

## Answer

**A) 1-Chloro-2,2-dimethylpropane**
The longest continuous carbon chain contains three carbon atoms (propane) with a chlorine atom at C-1 and two methyl groups at C-2.

*Source: NCERT, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*
