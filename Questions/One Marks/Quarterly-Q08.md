Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

8. What is Q in the following reaction?
Benzene --[Cl₂/FeCl₃]--> P --[Na/ether]--> Q

- A) Chlorobenzene
- B) Toluene
- C) Biphenyl
- D) Phenol

## Answer

**C) Biphenyl**
Electrophilic chlorination of benzene yields chlorobenzene (P). Reaction of chlorobenzene with sodium in dry ether (Fittig reaction) yields biphenyl (Q).

*Source: NCERT, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*
