Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

4. Identify the product formed when propan-1-ol is treated with alkaline KMnO₄ followed by acidification:

- A) Propanal
- B) Propanone
- C) Propanoic acid
- D) Propene

## Answer

**C) Propanoic acid**
Alkaline KMnO₄ is a strong oxidizing agent that oxidizes primary alcohols directly to carboxylic acids with the same number of carbon atoms.
