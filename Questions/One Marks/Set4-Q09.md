Chapter: Electrochemistry

9. Consider the following cell at 298 K :
Mg (s) | Mg²⁺ (1.0 M) || Cu²⁺ (1.0 M) | Cu (s)
How can we increase the emf of the cell using the same substances ?

- A) By decreasing only the [Mg²⁺] to 0.1 M
- B) By decreasing only the [Cu²⁺] to 0.1 M
- C) By increasing both [Mg²⁺] and [Cu²⁺] to 2.0 M
- D) By increasing only the [Mg²⁺] to 2.0 M

## Answer

**A) By decreasing only the [Mg²⁺] to 0.1 M**
According to the Nernst equation, Ecell = E°cell - (0.0591 / 2) log([Mg²⁺] / [Cu²⁺]); decreasing the anode ion concentration [Mg²⁺] lowers the logarithmic term and increases Ecell.
