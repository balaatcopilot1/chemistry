Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

14. Assertion (A): Phenol is less acidic than ethanol.
Reason (R): The phenoxide ion is stabilised by resonance.

- A) Assertion and reason are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is a correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement.

## Answer

**D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement.**
Phenol is substantially more acidic (pKa ~ 10) than ethanol (pKa ~ 16) because delocalization of the negative charge over the benzene ring stabilizes the phenoxide ion relative to the un-stabilized ethoxide ion.

*Source: NCERT, Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers*
