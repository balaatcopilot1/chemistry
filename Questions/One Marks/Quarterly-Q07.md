Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

7. Which of the following alkyl halides will undergo SN1 reaction most readily?

- A) (CH₃)₃C–F
- B) (CH₃)₃C–Cl
- C) (CH₃)₃C–Br
- D) (CH₃)₃C–I

## Answer

**D) (CH₃)₃C–I**
Iodide (I⁻) is the best leaving group due to its large size and weak C–I bond dissociation energy, making (CH₃)₃C–I react fastest via SN1.
