Chapter: Coordination Compounds

3. For an octahedral complex with a d⁶ metal ion, the electronic configuration in a strong ligand field (where Δ⒪ > P) will be:

- A) t₂g⁴ eg²
- B) t₂g⁶ eg⁰
- C) t₂g³ eg³
- D) t₂g⁵ eg¹

## Answer

**B) t₂g⁶ eg⁰**
In a strong ligand field where Δ⒪ > P, all six d-electrons pair up in the lower-energy t₂g orbitals before any electron populates the higher-energy eg orbitals.
