Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

6. Which of the following statement is not correct for the catalyst?

- A) It catalyses the forward and backward reaction to the same extent.
- B) It alters ΔG for any reaction.
- C) It is a substance that does not change the equilibrium constant of a reaction.
- D) It provides an alternative path by reducing activation energy between reactants and products.

## Answer

**B) It alters ΔG for any reaction.**
A catalyst speeds up the reaction rate by lowering the activation energy barrier but does not alter the thermodynamic Gibbs free energy change (ΔG) or equilibrium constant of the reaction.
