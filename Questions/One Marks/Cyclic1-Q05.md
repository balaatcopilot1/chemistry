5. Mass by volume percentage (w/V) is commonly used in:

- A) Metallurgy
- B) Medicine and pharmacy
- C) Agriculture
- D) Electronics

## Answer

**B) Medicine and pharmacy**
Mass by volume percentage (w/V) is routinely used in medicine and pharmacy to quantify solid drug preparations dissolved in liquid volumes.
