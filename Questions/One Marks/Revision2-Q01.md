1. The table below shows the KH values for some gases at 293 K and at same pressure:

KH values (kbar): 144.97, 69.16, 76.48, 34.86
Gas: Helium, Hydrogen, Nitrogen, Oxygen

In which of the following are the gases arranged in their decreasing order of solubility (from left to right)?

- A) Helium > Nitrogen > Hydrogen > Oxygen
- B) Nitrogen > Hydrogen > Oxygen > Helium
- C) Oxygen > Hydrogen > Nitrogen > Helium
- D) Hydrogen > Helium > Nitrogen > Oxygen

## Answer

**C) Oxygen > Hydrogen > Nitrogen > Helium**
According to Henry's Law (p = KH · x), solubility (x) is inversely proportional to Henry's Law constant (KH) at a given pressure, so lower KH values correspond to higher solubility.
