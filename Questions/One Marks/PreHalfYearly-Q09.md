Chapter: Amines

9. The best reagent for converting 2-phenylpropanamide to 1-phenylethanamine is:

- A) H₂ / Ni
- B) Na(Hg) / C₂H₅OH
- C) LiAlH₄
- D) NaOH / Br₂

## Answer

**D) NaOH / Br₂**
Hofmann bromamide degradation reaction converts an amide (C₆H₅CH(CH₃)CONH₂) to a primary amine (C₆H₅CH(CH₃)NH₂) containing one fewer carbon atom using Br₂ and aqueous NaOH.
