1. A student observes that a small amount of camphor disappears when kept in a closed container filled with nitrogen gas. Based on this, the student concludes the type of solution formed. Which of the following correctly identifies this system?

- A) Liquid solution with gas as solute
- B) Solid solution with solid as solvent
- C) Gaseous solution with solid as solute
- D) Liquid solution with solid as solute

## Answer

**C) Gaseous solution with solid as solute**
When solid camphor dissolves in nitrogen gas, gas acts as the solvent and solid as the solute, forming a gaseous solution.
