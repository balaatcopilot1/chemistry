1. Given below is an image showing a specific property of transition metals.
Which property of transition metals is shown in the image?

- A) Catalytic action
- B) Formation of alloy
- C) Coloured complex formation
- D) Molecular compounds

## Answer

**B) Formation of alloy**
Transition metals have similar atomic radii (within ~15% of each other), allowing atoms of one metal to easily replace atoms of another metal in the crystal lattice to form alloys.
