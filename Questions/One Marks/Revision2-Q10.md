Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

10. Rate of dehydration of alcohols follows the order:

- A) 2° > 1° > CH₃OH > 3°
- B) 3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃OH
- C) 2° > 3° > 1° > CH₃OH
- D) CH₃OH > 1° > 2° > 3°

## Answer

**B) 3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃OH**
Dehydration of alcohols proceeds via a carbocation intermediate; tertiary (3°) carbocations are the most stable, resulting in the highest rate of dehydration.
