Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

3. Generally transition elements form coloured salts due to the presence of unpaired electrons. Which of the following compounds will be coloured in the solid state?

- A) Ag₂SO₄
- B) CuF₂
- C) ZnF₂
- D) CdCl₂

## Answer

**B) CuF₂**
Cu²⁺ has a 3d⁹ electronic configuration with one unpaired electron capable of d-d transitions, whereas Ag⁺, Zn²⁺, and Cd²⁺ have completely filled d¹⁰ configurations and are colourless.
