Chapter: Electrochemistry

4. The charge required for the reduction of 1 mol of MnO₄⁻ to MnO₂ is

- A) 1 F
- B) 3 F
- C) 5 F
- D) 6 F

## Answer

**B) 3 F**
In MnO₄⁻ to MnO₂, the oxidation state of Mn changes from +7 to +4, requiring a transfer of 3 moles of electrons (3 Faraday) per mole of MnO₄⁻.
