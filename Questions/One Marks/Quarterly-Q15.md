Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

15. Assertion : CHCl₃ is stored in dark bottles.
Reason : CHCl₃ is oxidised in dark.

- A) Assertion and reason both are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement

## Answer

**C) Assertion is correct statement but reason is wrong statement.**
Chloroform is stored in dark bottles to prevent light exposure; it is slowly oxidised by air in the presence of light (not in the dark) to form toxic phosgene gas.
