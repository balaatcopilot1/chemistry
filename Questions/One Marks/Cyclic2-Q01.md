Chapter: Coordination Compounds

1. The magnetic moment of a certain octahedral complex [FeL₆]³⁻ is found to be 1.73 BM. This indicates that the ligand 'L' is:

- A) A weak field ligand causing sp³d² hybridization
- B) A strong field ligand causing d²sp³ hybridization
- C) A weak field ligand causing d²sp³ hybridization
- D) A strong field ligand causing sp³d² hybridization

## Answer

**B) A strong field ligand causing d²sp³ hybridization**
A magnetic moment of 1.73 BM corresponds to 1 unpaired electron (n = 1), which means Fe³⁺ (3d⁵) undergoes electron pairing driven by a strong field ligand, resulting in d²sp³ inner orbital hybridization.

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
