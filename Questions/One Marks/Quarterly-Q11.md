Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

11. Benzophenone can be obtained by

- A) Benzoyl chloride + benzene + AlCl₃
- B) Benzoyl chloride + diphenylcadmium
- C) benzoyl chloride + methylmagnesium chloride
- D) benzene + carbon monoxide + ZnCl₂

## Answer

**A) Benzoyl chloride + benzene + AlCl₃**
Benzoyl chloride reacts with benzene in the presence of anhydrous AlCl₃ catalyst (Friedel-Crafts acylation) to yield benzophenone (C₆H₅COC₆H₅).
