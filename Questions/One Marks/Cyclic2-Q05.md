Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

5. Arrange the following compounds in the decreasing order of their boiling points:
Butan-1-ol (I), Butan-2-ol (II), Ethoxyethane (III), Pentane (IV)

- A) I > II > III > IV
- B) IV > III > II > I
- C) I > III > II > IV
- D) II > I > III > IV

## Answer

**A) I > II > III > IV**
Primary alcohols (I) form stronger intermolecular hydrogen bonds than secondary alcohols (II), while ethers (III) have weak dipole interactions and alkanes (IV) possess only weak van der Waals dispersion forces.

*Source: NCERT, Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers*
