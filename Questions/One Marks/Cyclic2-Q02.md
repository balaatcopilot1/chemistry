Chapter: Coordination Compounds

2. Which of the following formulas correctly represents the coordination compound amminebromidochloridonitrito-N-platinate(II)?

- A) [Pt(NH₃)BrCl(NO₂)]⁻
- B) [Pt(NH₃)BrCl(ONO)]⁻
- C) [Pt(NH₃)₂BrCl(NO₂)]
- D) [Pt(NH₃)BrCl(NO₂)]⁺

## Answer

**A) [Pt(NH₃)BrCl(NO₂)]⁻**
The complex consists of Pt²⁺ coordinated to NH₃, Br⁻, Cl⁻, and NO₂⁻, giving a net complex charge of +2 - 1 - 1 - 1 = -1.

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
