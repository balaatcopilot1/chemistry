7. Assertion (A): Molarity of a solution does not change with temperature.
Reason (R): Volume of a solution depends on temperature, whereas mass does not.

- A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
- B) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
- C) (A) is correct but (R) is wrong
- D) (A) is wrong but (R) is correct

## Answer

**D) (A) is wrong but (R) is correct**
Molarity is defined per unit volume of solution, which changes with thermal expansion or contraction, making molarity temperature-dependent.
