14. Assertion: Order of the reaction can be zero or fractional
Reason: We cannot determine order from balanced chemical equation

- A) Assertion and reason both are correct statements and reason is correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason both are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement

## Answer

**B) Assertion and reason both are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.**
Order is an experimentally determined quantity that can be zero or fractional, and it cannot be deduced from balanced stoichiometry, but the inability to deduce it from stoichiometry is not the cause of fractional values.
