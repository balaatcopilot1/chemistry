Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

16. Assertion (A) : E° (Sc³⁺ / Sc²⁺) has low value.
Reason (R) : Because of the stability of Sc³⁺ ion which has a noble gas configuration.

- A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).
- B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).
- C) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.
- D) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.

## Answer

**A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).**
The low E° value for Sc³⁺ / Sc²⁺ reflects the exceptional stability of Sc³⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁰ configuration), which resists reduction back to Sc²⁺.

*Source: NCERT, The d- and f-Block Elements*
