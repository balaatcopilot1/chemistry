Chapter: Coordination Compounds

4. The chelating ligand used for the treatment of lead poisoning is :

- A) Ethane-1,2-diamine
- B) Oxalate ion
- C) Dimethylglyoxime
- D) EDTA

## Answer

**D) EDTA**
EDTA (Ethylenediaminetetraacetate) forms a soluble, stable chelate complex with toxic lead (Pb²⁺) ions, enabling them to be safely excreted from the body.

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
