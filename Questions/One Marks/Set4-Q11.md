Chapter: Biomolecules

11. Nucleic acids are polymers of :

- A) amino acids
- B) nucleosides
- C) nucleotides
- D) pentose sugar

## Answer

**C) nucleotides**
Nucleic acids (DNA and RNA) are long-chain biopolymers composed of repeating monomeric units called nucleotides, each consisting of a nitrogenous base, a pentose sugar, and a phosphate group.
