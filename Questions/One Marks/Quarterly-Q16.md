Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

16. Assertion : The bond angle in alcohols is slightly less than the tetrahedral angle.
Reason : In alcohols, the oxygen of –OH group is attached to sp³ hybridized carbon atoms.

- A) Assertion and reason both are correct statements and reason is the correct explanation for assertion.
- B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.
- C) Assertion is correct statement but reason is wrong statement.
- D) Assertion is wrong statement but reason is correct statement

## Answer

**B) Assertion and reason are correct statements but reason is not correct explanation for assertion.**
The C–O–H bond angle in alcohols (108.9°) is slightly less than tetrahedral (109.5°) due to lone pair-lone pair repulsion on the oxygen atom, not simply because oxygen is attached to an sp³ carbon.
