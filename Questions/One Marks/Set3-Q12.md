12. On hydrolysis, which of the following carbohydrates gives only glucose ?

- A) Sucrose
- B) Galactose
- C) Lactose
- D) Maltose

## Answer

**D) Maltose**
Maltose is a disaccharide composed of two α-D-glucose units linked by a C1–C4 glycosidic bond, yielding exclusively D-glucose upon acid or enzymatic hydrolysis.
