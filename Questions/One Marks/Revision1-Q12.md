Chapter: Biomolecules

12. Which of the following polymer is stored in the liver of animals?

- A) Glycogen
- B) Amylose
- C) Amylopectin
- D) Cellulose

## Answer

**A) Glycogen**
Glycogen is a branched polysaccharide stored in animal tissues, primarily in the liver and muscles, as an energy reserve.
