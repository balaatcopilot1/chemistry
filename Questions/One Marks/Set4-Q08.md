Chapter: Solutions

8. When a liquid and its vapour are at equilibrium and the pressure is suddenly decreased :

- A) vapour pressure of the solution increases
- B) heating occurs
- C) cooling occurs
- D) equilibrium remains unaffected

## Answer

**C) cooling occurs**
When pressure is suddenly reduced at equilibrium, more liquid evaporates to restore vapour pressure; since evaporation is an endothermic process that absorbs latent heat, cooling occurs.
