9. The mole fraction of a solute in 2·0 molal aqueous solution is :

- A) 1·87
- B) 0·347
- C) 0·0347
- D) 0·00347

## Answer

**C) 0·0347**
A 2.0 molal aqueous solution contains 2.0 mol solute in 1000 g water (55.55 mol H₂O); mole fraction x = 2.0 / (2.0 + 55.55) = 2.0 / 57.55 = 0.0347.
