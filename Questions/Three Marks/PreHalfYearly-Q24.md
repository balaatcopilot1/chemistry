Chapter: Solutions

24. (A) What mass of ethylene glycol (molar mass = 62.0 g mol⁻¹) must be added to 5.50 kg of water to lower the freezing point of water from 0 °C to -10 °C (Kf of water = 1.86 K kg mol⁻¹)?
OR
(B) A solution 0.1M of Na₂SO₄ is dissolved to the extent of 95%. What would be its osmotic pressure at 27 °C? (R = 0.0821 atm L K⁻¹ mol⁻¹)

## Answer

(A) Freezing Point Calculation:
Given: ΔTf = 10 °C = 10 K, Kf = 1.86 K kg mol⁻¹, w_solvent = 5.50 kg, M_solute = 62.0 g mol⁻¹.
Formula: ΔTf = (Kf × w_solute) / (M_solute × w_solvent_kg)
Substitution: 10 = (1.86 × w_solute) / (62.0 × 5.50)
10 = (1.86 × w_solute) / 341.0
w_solute = (10 × 341.0) / 1.86 = 3410 / 1.86 = 1833.33 g (or 1.833 kg).

OR

(B) Osmotic Pressure Calculation:
Given: C = 0.1 M, α = 0.95, T = 27 °C = 300 K, R = 0.0821 atm L K⁻¹ mol⁻¹.
Dissociation of Na₂SO₄: Na₂SO₄(aq) → 2 Na⁺(aq) + SO₄²⁻(aq) (n = 3 ions).
van 't Hoff factor: i = 1 + (n - 1)α = 1 + (3 - 1)(0.95) = 1 + 1.90 = 2.90.
Formula: π = i C R T
Substitution: π = 2.90 × 0.1 mol L⁻¹ × 0.0821 atm L K⁻¹ mol⁻¹ × 300 K = 7.1427 atm.
