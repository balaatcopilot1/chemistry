Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

18. Give reasons for the following statements:
(i) Why must a Grignard reagent be completely protected from even traces of moisture?
(ii) Why is it not possible to prepare aryl halides by treating phenol with HCl in the presence of ZnCl₂?
(iii) Why does allyl chloride undergo hydrolysis faster than n-propyl chloride?

## Answer

(i) Grignard reagents (RMgX) are highly reactive organometallic compounds. They react with any proton source such as water to form hydrocarbons:
RMgX + H₂O → R–H + Mg(OH)X
Hence, strictly anhydrous conditions are necessary.

(ii) In phenol, the C–O bond acquires partial double bond character due to resonance delocalization of unshared electron pairs of oxygen into the aromatic ring. This partial double bond is shorter, stronger, and difficult to cleave by nucleophilic substitution with HCl.

(iii) Allyl chloride (CH₂=CH–CH₂Cl) ionizes to form an allylic carbocation (CH₂=CH–CH₂⁺), which is stabilized by resonance. n-Propyl chloride forms a primary carbocation lacking resonance stabilization. Consequently, allyl chloride undergoes faster hydrolysis via the SN1 pathway.

*Source: NCERT, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*
