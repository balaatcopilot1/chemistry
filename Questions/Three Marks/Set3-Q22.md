Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

22. The half-life period of a radioactive element is 1·5 × 10¹⁰ years. Calculate the time in which the activity of the element is reduced to 75% of its original value. [Given : log 2 = 0·30, log 3 = 0·48, log 4 = 0·60]

## Answer

(a) First-order rate constant k = 0.693 / t₁/₂ = (2.303 × log 2) / (1.5 × 10¹⁰ years) = 4.62 × 10⁻¹¹ year⁻¹
(b) For reduction to 75% activity ([A]t = 0.75 [A]₀):
t = (2.303 / k) log ([A]₀ / [A]t) = (t₁/₂ / log 2) × log (100 / 75)
t = (1.5 × 10¹⁰ / 0.30) × (log 4 - log 3) = (5.0 × 10⁹) × (0.60 - 0.48) = (5.0 × 10⁹) × 0.12 = 6.0 × 10⁹ years.

Answer: 6.0 × 10⁹ years.
