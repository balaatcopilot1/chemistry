Chapter: Coordination Compounds

25. (a) When a coordination compound CoCl₃ . 6NH₃ is mixed with excess of AgNO₃ solution, 3 moles of AgCl are precipitated per mole of the compound. Write the structural formula of the complex, IUPAC name, its hybridisation and magnetic behaviour on the basis of valence bond theory.
OR
(b) (i) How many geometrical isomers are possible in each of the following complexes ?
1. [Cr(C₂O₄)₃]³⁻
2. [Co(NH₃)₃Cl₃]
(ii) [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ is an inner orbital complex whereas [Ni(NH₃)₆]²⁺ is an outer orbital complex. Why ? [Atomic number : Co = 27, Ni = 28]
(iii) Write the formula of Wilkinson catalyst and its use.

## Answer

(a) Structural Formula: [Co(NH₃)₆]Cl₃
IUPAC Name: Hexaamminecobalt(III) chloride
Hybridisation & Magnetic Behaviour:
Co³⁺ has a 3d⁶ configuration. Strong field NH₃ ligands pair up 3d electrons into t₂g, leaving two 3d orbitals vacant for d²sp³ hybridisation. Since all 6 d-electrons are paired, the complex is diamagnetic.

OR

(b) (i) 1. [Cr(C₂O₄)₃]³⁻: 0 geometrical isomers (symmetrical tris-bidentate complex).
2. [Co(NH₃)₃Cl₃]: 2 geometrical isomers (facial/fac and meridional/mer).

(ii) Co³⁺ (3d⁶) with strong field NH₃ pairs up electrons to provide inner 3d orbitals for d²sp³ hybridisation (inner orbital complex). Ni²⁺ (3d⁸) has 8 electrons in 3d; pairing leaves only one vacant 3d orbital (insufficient for two inner 3d orbitals), so it uses outer 4d orbitals forming sp³d² (outer orbital complex).

(iii) Formula: [Rh(PPh₃)₃Cl] (Chloridotris(triphenylphosphine)rhodium(I))
Use: Homogeneous hydrogenation of alkenes.

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
