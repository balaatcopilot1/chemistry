Chapter: Amines

27. How will you obtain the following from benzenediazonium chloride ? Give chemical equations involved :
(a) Chlorobenzene
(b) Benzene
(c) Benzonitrile

## Answer

(a) Sandmeyer Reaction: Reaction with Cu₂Cl₂ and HCl yields chlorobenzene:
C₆H₅N₂⁺Cl⁻ --[Cu₂Cl₂ / HCl]--> C₆H₅Cl + N₂↑ + CuCl
(b) Deamination: Reduction with hypophosphorous acid (H₃PO₂) in the presence of Cu⁺ yields benzene:
C₆H₅N₂⁺Cl⁻ + H₃PO₂ + H₂O --[Cu⁺]--> C₆H₆ + N₂↑ + H₃PO₃ + HCl
(c) Nitrile Synthesis: Reaction with CuCN/KCN yields benzonitrile:
C₆H₅N₂⁺Cl⁻ + CuCN --[KCN]--> C₆H₅CN + N₂↑ + CuCl
