Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

16. Write the equations involved in the following reactions:
(i) HVZ reaction.
(ii) Gattermann-Koch reaction
(iii) Aldol condensation

## Answer

(i) Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (HVZ) reaction:
Carboxylic acids containing α-hydrogens are halogenated at the α-position upon treatment with chlorine or bromine in the presence of red phosphorus, followed by hydrolysis.

Equation:
CH₃COOH(l) + Br₂(l) --[red P / H₂O]--> ClCH₂COOH(l) + HBr(g)

(ii) Gattermann-Koch reaction:
Benzene reacts with carbon monoxide and hydrogen chloride gas in the presence of anhydrous aluminium chloride and cuprous chloride catalyst to yield benzaldehyde.

Equation:
C₆H₆(l) + CO(g) + HCl(g) --[anhydrous AlCl₃ / CuCl]--> C₆H₅CHO(l) + HCl(g)

(iii) Aldol condensation:
Aldehydes or ketones having at least one α-hydrogen undergo self-condensation in the presence of dilute alkali to form β-hydroxyaldehydes (aldols), which upon heating lose water to form α,β-unsaturated carbonyl compounds.

Equation:
2 CH₃CHO(l) --[dilute NaOH]--> CH₃–CH(OH)–CH₂–CHO(l) --[heat, -H₂O]--> CH₃–CH=CH–CHO(l) [But-2-enal]

*Source: NCERT, Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids*
