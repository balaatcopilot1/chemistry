Chapter: Coordination Compounds

23. Draw all the isomers of: (geometrical and optical)
i) [CoCl₂(en)₂]⁺
ii) [Co(NH₃)Cl(en)₂]²⁺
iii) [Co(NH₃)₂Cl₂(en)]⁺

## Answer

(i) [CoCl₂(en)₂]⁺:
- Cis-isomer (chiral): Exists as non-superimposable d- and l-enantiomers (optical isomers).
- Trans-isomer (achiral): Has a plane of symmetry and is optically inactive.
Total Isomers = 3 (cis-d, cis-l, and trans).

(ii) [Co(NH₃)Cl(en)₂]²⁺:
- Cis-isomer: Optically active, exists as d- and l-enantiomers.
- Trans-isomer: Optically inactive due to plane of symmetry.
Total Isomers = 3 (cis-d, cis-l, and trans).

(iii) [Co(NH₃)₂Cl₂(en)]⁺:
- Geometrical Isomers: Cis-Cl/cis-NH₃, cis-Cl/trans-NH₃, trans-Cl/cis-NH₃.
- Optical Activity: Cis-Cl/cis-NH₃ form is chiral and exhibits optical isomerism (d- and l-enantiomers).
