Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

24. How can you convert the following?
i) But-1-ene to 1-iodobutane
ii) Benzene to acetophenone
iii) Ethanol to propanenitrile

## Answer

(i) But-1-ene to 1-iodobutane:
Step 1: Treat but-1-ene with HBr in the presence of peroxide (anti-Markovnikov addition) to obtain 1-bromobutane.
CH₃CH₂CH=CH₂ + HBr --[peroxide]--> CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂Br
Step 2: React 1-bromobutane with NaI in dry acetone (Finkelstein reaction) to yield 1-iodobutane.
CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂Br + NaI --[dry acetone]--> CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂I + NaBr

(ii) Benzene to Acetophenone:
React benzene with acetyl chloride in the presence of anhydrous AlCl₃ catalyst (Friedel-Crafts acylation).
C₆H₆ + CH₃COCl --[anhydrous AlCl₃]--> C₆H₅COCH₃ + HCl

(iii) Ethanol to Propanenitrile:
Step 1: Convert ethanol to ethyl chloride using thionyl chloride (SOCl₂).
CH₃CH₂OH + SOCl₂ → CH₃CH₂Cl + SO₂↑ + HCl↑
Step 2: Treat ethyl chloride with KCN in aqueous ethanol to obtain propanenitrile.
CH₃CH₂Cl + KCN(aq) → CH₃CH₂CN + KCl
