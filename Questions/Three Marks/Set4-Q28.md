Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

28. Answer the following :
(a) Which isomer of C₄H₉Br is most reactive towards SN1 reaction ?
(b) Predict the alkene that would be formed by dehydrohalogenation of 1-Bromo-1-methylcyclohexane.
(c) Although chlorine shows strong – I effect, yet it is ortho- and para-directing in electrophilic aromatic substitution reactions. Why ?

## Answer

(a) 2-Bromo-2-methylpropane (tert-butyl bromide, (CH₃)₃C–Br) is most reactive towards SN1 reaction because it forms a stable 3° carbocation intermediate.

(b) According to Saytzeff's rule, dehydrohalogenation of 1-bromo-1-methylcyclohexane with alcoholic KOH yields 1-methylcyclohexene as the major alkene product (more substituted, highly stable double bond).

(c) Chlorine exerts a strong electron-withdrawing inductive (-I) effect, but its unshared electron pairs delocalize into the ring via resonance (+R effect), specifically increasing electron density at ortho and para positions relative to meta position.

*Source: NCERT, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*
