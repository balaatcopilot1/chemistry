Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

28. Account for the following:
(A) Why transition metals form complex compounds?
(B) Why first ionisation enthalpy of Cr is lower than that of Zn?
(C) Out of Cu₂Cl₂ and CuCl₂, which is more stable and why?

## Answer

(a) Complex Formation:
Transition metals form complexes due to: (1) small ionic sizes, (2) high nuclear charge, and (3) availability of vacant d-orbitals to accept electron pairs from ligands.

(b) First Ionization Enthalpy Comparison:
Cr ([Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹) loses one 4s electron to achieve a stable half-filled 3d⁵ configuration, requiring lower ionization energy. Zn ([Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s²) has a fully-filled 4s subshell requiring higher energy to break.

(c) Stability of CuCl₂ vs Cu₂Cl₂:
CuCl₂ (containing Cu²⁺) is more stable than Cu₂Cl₂ (containing Cu⁺) in aqueous solution because the high hydration enthalpy of Cu²⁺ overcompensates for the second ionization energy of copper.
