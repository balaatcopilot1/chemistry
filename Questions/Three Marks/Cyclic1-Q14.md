Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

14. Explain the following reactions with suitable equations:
a) Wurtz–Fittig reaction
b) Friedel–Crafts reaction

## Answer

(a) Wurtz–Fittig reaction:
A mixture of an alkyl halide and an aryl halide reacts with sodium metal in dry ether to form an alkylarene.

Equation:
C₆H₅–Cl(l) + 2 Na(s) + Cl–CH₃(l) --[dry ether]--> C₆H₅–CH₃(l) + 2 NaCl(s)

(b) Friedel–Crafts reaction:
Haloarenes undergo electrophilic aromatic substitution when treated with an alkyl halide in the presence of anhydrous aluminium chloride (AlCl₃) catalyst to form alkyl-substituted haloarenes.

Equation (Friedel–Crafts Alkylation):
C₆H₅–Cl(l) + CH₃–Cl(l) --[anhydrous AlCl₃]--> 1-chloro-4-methylbenzene(l) [major] + 1-chloro-2-methylbenzene(l) [minor] + HCl(g)
