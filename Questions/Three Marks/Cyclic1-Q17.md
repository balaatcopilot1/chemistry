17. Calculate the mass percentage of benzene (C₆H₆) and carbon tetrachloride (CCl₄) if 22 g of benzene is dissolved in 122 g of carbon tetrachloride.

## Answer

Given:
Mass of benzene (C₆H₆) = 22 g
Mass of carbon tetrachloride (CCl₄) = 122 g

Total mass of solution = 22 g + 122 g = 144 g

Formula:
Mass percentage of component = (Mass of component / Total mass of solution) × 100

Calculations:
(i) Mass percentage of benzene = (22 / 144) × 100 = 15.28%
(ii) Mass percentage of CCl₄ = (122 / 144) × 100 = 84.72%

Answer:
Mass percentage of benzene = 15.28%
Mass percentage of CCl₄ = 84.72%
