Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

16. Explain the reactions of chlorobenzene with
(i) halogens, anhydrous AlCl₃
(ii) nitrating mixture, and
(iii) concentrated sulphuric acid,
giving equations and identifying the major and minor products formed.

## Answer

(i) Halogenation: Chlorobenzene reacts with chlorine in the presence of anhydrous AlCl₃ catalyst to form 1,4-dichlorobenzene as the major product and 1,2-dichlorobenzene as the minor product.
Equation:
C₆H₅–Cl(l) + Cl₂(g) --[anhydrous AlCl₃]--> 1,4-dichlorobenzene(s) [major] + 1,2-dichlorobenzene(l) [minor] + HCl(g)

(ii) Nitration: Chlorobenzene reacts with a nitrating mixture of concentrated HNO₃ and concentrated H₂SO₄ to produce 1-chloro-4-nitrobenzene as the major product and 1-chloro-2-nitrobenzene as the minor product.
Equation:
C₆H₅–Cl(l) + HNO₃(l) --[concentrated H₂SO₄]--> 1-chloro-4-nitrobenzene(s) [major] + 1-chloro-2-nitrobenzene(l) [minor] + H₂O(l)

(iii) Sulphonation: Chlorobenzene reacts with concentrated H₂SO₄ upon heating to give 4-chlorobenzenesulphonic acid as the major product and 2-chlorobenzenesulphonic acid as the minor product.
Equation:
C₆H₅–Cl(l) + H₂SO₄(l) --[heat]--> 4-chlorobenzenesulphonic acid(s) [major] + 2-chlorobenzenesulphonic acid(l) [minor] + H₂O(l)

*Source: NCERT, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*
