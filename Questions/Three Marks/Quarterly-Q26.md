Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

26. An organic compound contains 69.77% carbon, 11.63 % hydrogen and rest oxygen. The molecular mass of the compound is 86. It does not reduce Tollen's reagent but forms an addition compound with sodium hydrogensulphite and gives a positive iodoform test. On vigorous oxidation, it gives ethanoic and propanoic acid. Write the possible structure of the compound.

## Answer

Step 1: Determine Empirical and Molecular Formula:
- % Carbon = 69.77, Moles = 69.77 / 12 = 5.81
- % Hydrogen = 11.63, Moles = 11.63 / 1 = 11.63
- % Oxygen = 100 - (69.77 + 11.63) = 18.60%, Moles = 18.60 / 16 = 1.16
Ratio C : H : O = 5.81/1.16 : 11.63/1.16 : 1.16/1.16 = 5 : 10 : 1
Empirical Formula = C₅H₁₀O (Empirical mass = 86 g mol⁻¹)
Given Molecular Mass = 86, so Molecular Formula is C₅H₁₀O.

Step 2: Functional Group Deduction:
- Does not reduce Tollens' reagent → Compound is a ketone (not an aldehyde).
- Forms NaHSO₃ adduct and positive iodoform test → Contains a methyl ketone group (CH₃–CO–).
- Vigorous oxidation yields ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH) and propanoic acid (CH₃CH₂COOH) → Carbonyl group is at C-2 of a 5-carbon straight chain.

Structure:
Pentan-2-one (CH₃–CO–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃)
