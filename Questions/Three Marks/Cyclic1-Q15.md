Chapter: Solutions

15. Explain the following concentration terms:
(a) Parts per million (ppm)
(b) Mole fraction

## Answer

(a) Parts per million (ppm):
It is used to express concentration when a solute is present in trace quantities. It is defined as the number of parts of a component per million (10⁶) total parts of all components in the solution.

Formula:
ppm of A = (Mass of component A / Total mass of solution) × 10⁶

(b) Mole fraction:
Mole fraction (x) of a component is the ratio of the number of moles of that component to the total number of moles of all components present in the solution.

Formula for component A in a binary mixture of A and B:
x_A = n_A / (n_A + n_B)

Mole fraction is dimensionless and the sum of mole fractions of all components in a solution is always equal to 1.
