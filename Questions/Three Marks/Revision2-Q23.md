Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

23. Consider the following reaction mechanism given below:
Step 1: 2X + Y → V (fast)
Step 2: V + C → D + L (slow)

i) What will be the overall order of the reaction?
ii) Write the unit of rate constant of the reaction and the rate law expression.

## Answer

(i) Overall Order of Reaction:
The rate-determining step is the slow step (Step 2): Rate = k_slow [V] [C].
From fast equilibrium Step 1: K = [V] / ([X]² [Y]) ⇒ [V] = K [X]² [Y].
Substituting [V] into slow rate law gives Rate = k' [X]² [Y] [C].
Overall Order = 2 + 1 + 1 = 4 (Fourth order).

(ii) Rate Law & Units:
- Rate Law Expression: Rate = k [X]² [Y] [C]
- Units of Rate Constant (k): L³ mol⁻³ s⁻¹ (or mol⁻³ L³ s⁻¹).
