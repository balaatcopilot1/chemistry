Chapter: Amines

25. (A) Why Friedel-Crafts reaction does not occur in aniline.
(B) Why Ethylamine is water soluble, but aniline is not.
(C) Why cannot aniline be prepared by Gabriel phthalimide synthesis

## Answer

(a) Aniline is a Lewis base and forms a salt with anhydrous AlCl₃ (Lewis acid catalyst), placing a positive charge on nitrogen (–NH₂⁺–AlCl₃⁻) which strongly deactivates the aromatic ring toward electrophilic substitution.

(b) Ethylamine forms strong hydrogen bonds with water molecules due to its small alkyl chain. In aniline, the large hydrophobic phenyl group (C₆H₅–) hinders hydrogen bonding, rendering aniline insoluble in water.

(c) Gabriel phthalimide synthesis involves nucleophilic substitution of phthalimide anion on an alkyl halide. Aryl halides like chlorobenzene do not undergo nucleophilic substitution under mild conditions due to partial double bond character of the C–Cl bond.

*Source: NCERT, Amines*
