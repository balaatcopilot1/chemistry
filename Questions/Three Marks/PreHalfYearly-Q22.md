22. For the reaction A + B → Products, the following rate data were obtained:

Exp. No | [A] (mol L⁻¹) | [B] (mol L⁻¹) | Initial Rate (mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹)
1 | 0.1 | 0.1 | 2.0 × 10⁻³
2 | 0.2 | 0.1 | 4.0 × 10⁻³
3 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 8.0 × 10⁻³

i) Determine the order of reaction with respect to A and B.
ii) Write the rate law equation.
iii) Calculate the rate constant (k).

## Answer

(i) Order of Reaction:
Let Rate = k [A]ˣ [B]ʸ
- Comparing Exp 1 & 2: [B] is constant, [A] doubles (0.1 → 0.2), rate doubles (2.0×10⁻³ → 4.0×10⁻³). Thus, 2ˣ = 2¹ ⇒ x = 1 (First order w.r.t A).
- Comparing Exp 1 & 3: [A] is constant, [B] doubles (0.1 → 0.2), rate quadruples (2.0×10⁻³ → 8.0×10⁻³). Thus, 2ʸ = 4 = 2² ⇒ y = 2 (Second order w.r.t B).

(ii) Rate Law Equation:
Rate = k [A]¹ [B]²

(iii) Rate Constant (k):
Using Exp 1: 2.0 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹ = k (0.1 mol L⁻¹) (0.1 mol L⁻¹)²
2.0 × 10⁻³ = k (1.0 × 10⁻³) ⇒ k = 2.0 L² mol⁻² s⁻¹.
