Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

27. a) Distinguish between the following:
(i) Benzaldehyde and butanal
(ii) Propanal and butan-2-one
b) Write a note on Cannizzaro reaction.

## Answer

(a) Chemical Tests:

(i) Benzaldehyde vs. Butanal: Fehling's Test
Butanal reduces Fehling's solution to form a red precipitate of Cu₂O. Benzaldehyde (aromatic aldehyde) does not reduce Fehling's solution.
CH₃CH₂CH₂CHO + 2 Cu²⁺ + 5 OH⁻ → CH₃CH₂CH₂COO⁻ + Cu₂O(s) [red ppt] + 3 H₂O

(ii) Propanal vs. Butan-2-one: Iodoform Test
Butan-2-one contains a CH₃CO– group and forms a yellow precipitate of iodoform (CHI₃) when warmed with I₂ and NaOH. Propanal does not give iodoform test.
CH₃COCH₂CH₃ + 3 I₂ + 4 NaOH → CHI₃(s) [yellow ppt] + CH₃CH₂COONa + 3 NaI + 3 H₂O

(b) Cannizzaro Reaction:
Aldehydes lacking an α-hydrogen atom undergo self-oxidation and reduction (disproportionation) when treated with concentrated alkali. One molecule is reduced to an alcohol while the other is oxidized to a carboxylic acid salt.

Equation:
2 HCHO + conc. NaOH → CH₃OH + HCOONa
