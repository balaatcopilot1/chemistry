Chapter: Electrochemistry

24. Answer the following questions :
(a) Why is direct current (DC) not used to measure the resistance of an ionic solution ?
(b) Why are the products of electrolysis different for the electrolysis of aqueous solution of AgNO₃ with silver electrodes and electrolysis of aqueous solution of AgNO₃ with platinum electrodes ?
(c) Why are magnesium blocks fixed to the iron pipelines carrying water ?

## Answer

(a) Direct current (DC) causes electrolysis of the ionic solution, changing its composition and concentration, and leads to polarization at the electrodes. Alternating current (AC) is used instead.

(b) With silver (attackable/active) electrodes, Ag anode dissolves into Ag⁺ ions while Ag⁺ ions deposit as Ag at the cathode. With inert platinum electrodes, Ag⁺ ions deposit at cathode while water is oxidized at anode to release O₂ gas (4 OH⁻ → O₂ + 2 H₂O + 4 e⁻).

(c) Magnesium has a more negative reduction potential than iron and acts as a sacrificial anode, undergoing preference oxidation to protect the iron underground pipelines from corrosion (cathodic protection).
