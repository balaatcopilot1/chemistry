Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

18. Explain the mechanism of the reaction where ethanol is converted to ethoxyethane at 413 K in the presence of concentrated sulphuric acid. Why is 443 K avoided for this specific synthesis?

## Answer

(a) Mechanism of Ethoxyethane Formation (SN2 pathway at 413 K):

Step 1: Protonation of ethanol molecule:
CH₃–CH₂–OH + H⁺ ⇌ CH₃–CH₂–OH₂⁺

Step 2: Nucleophilic attack by an unprotonated ethanol molecule on the protonated alcohol (slow step):
CH₃–CH₂–OH + CH₃–CH₂–OH₂⁺ → CH₃–CH₂–O⁺(H)–CH₂–CH₃ + H₂O

Step 3: Deprotonation to form ethoxyethane:
CH₃–CH₂–O⁺(H)–CH₂–CH₃ → CH₃–CH₂–O–CH₂–CH₃ + H⁺

(b) Reason 443 K is avoided:
At higher temperature (443 K), intramolecular dehydration predominates over intermolecular dehydration, converting ethanol into ethene (alkene) via elimination instead of ethoxyethane (ether).

Reaction at 443 K:
CH₃–CH₂–OH --[conc. H₂SO₄, 443 K]--> CH₂=CH₂ + H₂O
