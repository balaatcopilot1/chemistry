Chapter: Solutions

26. Calculate the vapour pressure of a solution containing 61 g of benzoic acid (molar mass = 122 g mol⁻¹) dissolved in 500 g of benzene when the vapour pressure of pure benzene at this temperature of experiment is 66 torr. Assume complete dimerization of benzoic acid in benzene.

## Answer

Given:
w_solute = 61 g, M_solute = 122 g mol⁻¹ ⇒ Moles of benzoic acid before association = 61 / 122 = 0.5 mol
For 100% dimerization (2 C₆H₅COOH ⇌ (C₆H₅COOH)₂), van 't Hoff factor i = 0.5.
Effective moles of solute = i × 0.5 = 0.25 mol.
w_solvent = 500 g, M_solvent = 78 g mol⁻¹ ⇒ Moles of benzene = 500 / 78 = 6.41 mol.

Mole fraction of solute (x_solute):
x_solute = 0.25 / (0.25 + 6.41) = 0.25 / 6.66 = 0.0375

Using Raoult's Law relative lowering of vapour pressure:
(p° - p) / p° = x_solute
(66 - p) / 66 = 0.0375
66 - p = 2.475 ⇒ p = 66 - 2.475 = 63.525 torr.

Answer: 63.525 torr (or ~63.53 torr).
