Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

27. How would you account for the following :
(A) Among lanthanoids, Ln (III) compounds are predominant. However, occasionally in solutions or in solid compounds, +2 and +4 ions are also obtained.
(B) The E° M²⁺/M for copper is positive (0.34 V). Copper is the only metal in the first series of transition elements showing this behaviour.
(C) Eu²⁺ is a strong reducing agent

## Answer

(a) +2 and +4 oxidation states occur when they result in extra-stable empty (4f⁰), half-filled (4f⁷), or fully-filled (4f¹⁴) electronic configurations (e.g., Eu²⁺ [4f⁷], Yb²⁺ [4f¹⁴], Ce⁴⁺ [4f⁰], Tb⁴⁺ [4f⁷]).

(b) The high enthalpy of atomization and high sum of first and second ionization enthalpies required to convert Cu(s) into Cu²⁺(aq) are not balanced by its hydration enthalpy, making E°(Cu²⁺/Cu) positive (+0.34 V).

(c) Eu²⁺ has configuration [Xe] 4f⁷. It readily oxidizes to Eu³⁺ ([Xe] 4f⁶), the predominant oxidation state in aqueous solution, making Eu²⁺ a strong reducing agent.
