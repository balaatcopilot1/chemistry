Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

28. An organic compound (A) with molecular formula C₃H₅N on reaction with C₆H₅MgBr followed by hydrolysis, gives a compound (B). Compound (B) forms an orange-red precipitate with 2,4-DNP reagent and does not give iodoform test. It neither reduces Tollens’ or Fehling’s reagent nor does it decolourise bromine water. On drastic oxidation with chromic acid it gives a carboxylic acid (C) having molecular formula C⇇H₆O₂. Identify the compounds (A), (B) and (C). Write the reactions of compound (A) with C₆H₅MgBr followed by hydrolysis to give compound (B).

## Answer

(a) Identification:
- Compound (A) = Propanenitrile [CH₃CH₂CN] (Formula C₃H₅N)
- Compound (B) = 1-Phenylpropan-1-one (Propiophenone) [C₆H₅COCH₂CH₃]
- Compound (C) = Benzoic acid [C₆H₅COOH] (Formula C⇇H₆O₂)
(b) Reactions:
Reaction of (A) with C₆H₅MgBr followed by hydrolysis:
CH₃CH₂CN + C₆H₅MgBr --[dry ether]--> CH₃CH₂C(=NMgBr)C₆H₅ --[H₃O⁺]--> C₆H₅COCH₂CH₃ (B) + Mg(OH)Br + NH₃
Drastic oxidation of (B) with chromic acid:
C₆H₅COCH₂CH₃ --[H₂CrO₄, heat]--> C₆H₅COOH (C) + CH₃COOH

*Source: NCERT, Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids*
