Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

15. Predict the products of the following reactions:
(i) CH₃–C(=O)–CH₃ + H₂N–NH₂ → ?
(ii) C₆H₅–CH₃ --[(a) KMnO₄/KOH, Δ (b) H⁺]--> ?
(iii) C₆H₅–COOH --[Br₂/FeBr₃]--> ?

## Answer

(i) Propan-2-one hydrazone
Equation:
CH₃–CO–CH₃(l) + H₂N–NH₂(aq) --[H⁺]--> CH₃–C(=N–NH₂)–CH₃(l) + H₂O(l)

(ii) Benzoic acid
Equation:
C₆H₅–CH₃(l) --[(a) KMnO₄/KOH, heat]--> C₆H₅–COOK(aq) --[(b) H⁺]--> C₆H₅–COOH(s)

(iii) 3-Bromobenzoic acid (m-bromobenzoic acid)
Equation:
C₆H₅–COOH(s) + Br₂(l) --[FeBr₃]--> m-Br–C₆H₄–COOH(s) + HBr(g)
(The carboxyl group –COOH is meta-directing and deactivating.)

*Source: NCERT, Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids*
