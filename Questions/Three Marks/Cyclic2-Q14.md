Chapter: Coordination Compounds

14. Using Valence Bond Theory (VBT), describe the hybridization, geometry, and magnetic behaviour of the [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻ ion. (Atomic number of Ni=28).

## Answer

(a) Oxidation State and Configuration:
Ni (Z = 28): [Ar] 3d⁸ 4s²
In [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻, Nickel is in +2 oxidation state (Ni²⁺): [Ar] 3d⁸ 4s⁰ 4p⁰

(b) Hybridization and Orbital Rearrangement:
Cyanide ion (CN⁻) is a strong field ligand. It causes pairing of the 3d electrons:
3d configuration after pairing: 4 paired 3d orbitals (3d⁸) leaving one 3d orbital vacant.
One 3d, one 4s, and two 4p orbitals hybridize to give four dsp² hybrid orbitals.

(c) Geometry and Magnetic Behaviour:
- Hybridization: dsp²
- Geometry: Square planar
- Magnetic Behaviour: Diamagnetic (all electrons are paired, 0 unpaired electrons)
