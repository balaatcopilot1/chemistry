Chapter: Coordination Compounds

16. Distinguish between Ionization isomerism and Hydrate isomerism with one example of each pair.

## Answer

(a) Ionization Isomerism:
This isomerism arises when the counter ion in a coordination compound is itself a potential ligand and can displace a ligand from the coordination sphere, giving different ions in aqueous solution.

Example Pair:
- [Co(NH₃)₅SO₄]Br (red-violet, gives yellow precipitate of AgBr with AgNO₃)
- [Co(NH₃)₅Br]SO₄ (red, gives white precipitate of BaSO₄ with BaCl₂)

(b) Hydrate (Solvate) Isomerism:
This isomerism occurs when water is involved as a solvent. The isomers differ in whether water molecules are directly bound to the metal ion as ligands or present as free solvent molecules in the crystal lattice.

Example Pair:
- [Cr(H₂O)₆]Cl₃ (violet)
- [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂·H₂O (grey-green)

*Source: NCERT, Coordination Compounds*
