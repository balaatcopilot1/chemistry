Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

25. i) Give reason for the following: p-dichlorobenzene has higher melting point than that of ortho or meta isomer.
ii) Out of chlorobenzene and benzyl chloride, which one gets easily hydrolysed by aqueous NaOH and why?
iii) Write the structure of an isomer of compound C₄H₉Br which is most reactive towards SN1 reaction.

## Answer

(i) p-Dichlorobenzene has a symmetrical structure that fits more closely and tightly into the crystal lattice than ortho or meta isomers. Consequently, strong intermolecular forces require more thermal energy to break the lattice, resulting in a higher melting point.

(ii) Benzyl chloride (C₆H₅CH₂Cl) is hydrolysed easily by aqueous NaOH. The C–Cl bond in chlorobenzene has partial double bond character due to resonance with the benzene ring, making it extremely difficult to cleave. In benzyl chloride, loss of Cl⁻ yields a benzylic carbocation (C₆H₅CH₂⁺) stabilized by resonance.

(iii) Structure of C₄H₉Br isomer most reactive towards SN1:
2-Bromo-2-methylpropane (tert-butyl bromide): (CH₃)₃C–Br
(Being a tertiary alkyl halide, it forms a highly stable tertiary carbocation intermediate).

*Source: NCERT, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*
