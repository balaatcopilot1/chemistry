Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

27. Consider the decomposition of hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) as per the equation given below :
2H₂O₂ --[I⁻ / Alkaline medium]--> 2H₂O + O₂
The mechanism for this reaction was found to be :
Step I : H₂O₂ + I⁻ → H₂O + IO⁻ (slow)
Step II : H₂O₂ + IO⁻ → H₂O + I⁻ + O₂ (fast)
(a) Write the rate law expression.
(b) Determine the order of reaction with respect to H₂O₂, I⁻ and overall order of reaction.
(c) What is the molecularity of the reaction in Step II ?

## Answer

(a) Rate Law Expression is governed by the slow rate-determining Step I:
Rate = k [H₂O₂] [I⁻]

(b) Order w.r.t. H₂O₂ = 1
Order w.r.t. I⁻ = 1
Overall Order = 1 + 1 = 2 (Second order)

(c) Molecularity of Step II is 2 (bimolecular reaction involving one H₂O₂ molecule and one IO⁻ ion).
