Chapter: Electrochemistry

18. Conductivity of 0.00241 M acetic acid is 7.896 × 10⁻⁵ S cm⁻¹. Calculate its molar conductivity. If Λm° for acetic acid is 390.5 S cm² mol⁻¹, what is its dissociation constant?

## Answer

Given:
Concentration, c = 0.00241 M = 0.00241 mol L⁻¹
Conductivity, κ = 7.896 × 10⁻⁵ S cm⁻¹
Limiting molar conductivity, Λm° = 390.5 S cm² mol⁻¹

Step 1: Calculate Molar Conductivity (Λm):
Formula: Λm = (κ × 1000) / c
Substitution:
Λm = (7.896 × 10⁻⁵ × 1000) / 0.00241
Λm = 0.07896 / 0.00241 = 32.76 S cm² mol⁻¹

Step 2: Calculate Degree of Dissociation (α):
Formula: α = Λm / Λm°
Substitution:
α = 32.76 / 390.5 = 0.0839

Step 3: Calculate Dissociation Constant (Ka):
Formula: Ka = (c × α²) / (1 - α)
Substitution:
Ka = [0.00241 × (0.0839)²] / (1 - 0.0839)
Ka = (0.00241 × 0.007039) / 0.9161 = 1.85 × 10⁻⁵ mol L⁻¹

Answer:
Molar conductivity (Λm) = 32.76 S cm² mol⁻¹
Dissociation constant (Ka) = 1.85 × 10⁻⁵ mol L⁻¹
