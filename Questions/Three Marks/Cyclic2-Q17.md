Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

17. Propose a chemical route to synthesize phenol starting from aniline. Include the intermediate diazotisation step and reaction conditions.

## Answer

Step 1: Diazotisation of Aniline
Aniline is treated with nitrous acid (prepared in situ from NaNO₂ and dilute HCl) at low temperature (273–278 K) to form benzenediazonium chloride.

Equation:
C₆H₅–NH₂(l) + NaNO₂(aq) + 2 HCl(aq) --[273–278 K]--> C₆H₅–N₂⁺Cl⁻(aq) + NaCl(aq) + 2 H₂O(l)

Step 2: Hydrolysis of Diazonium Salt
The benzenediazonium chloride solution is warmed with water or treated with dilute acid to yield phenol with liberation of nitrogen gas.

Equation:
C₆H₅–N₂⁺Cl⁻(aq) + H₂O(l) --[warm]--> C₆H₅–OH(aq) + N₂(g) + HCl(aq)

Overall Synthesis Route:
Aniline --[NaNO₂ + HCl, 273–278 K]--> Benzenediazonium chloride --[H₂O, warm]--> Phenol
