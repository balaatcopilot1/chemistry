Explain the following with chemical equations:

1. Carbylamine reaction
2. Hoffmann bromamide degradation

## Answer

1. **Carbylamine reaction:** a primary amine reacts with chloroform and alcoholic KOH to give a foul-smelling isocyanide.
   R–NH₂ + CHCl₃ + 3KOH → R–NC + 3KCl + 3H₂O
2. **Hoffmann bromamide degradation:** an amide is converted into a primary amine with one carbon fewer.
   R–CONH₂ + Br₂ + 4NaOH → R–NH₂ + Na₂CO₃ + 2NaBr + 2H₂O
