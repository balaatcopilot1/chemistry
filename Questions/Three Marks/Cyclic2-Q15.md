Chapter: Coordination Compounds

15. Draw the structures of the optical isomers (enantiomers) for the complex ion [Co(en)₃]³⁺. Why is the trans-isomer of [CoCl₂(en)₂]⁺ optically inactive?

## Answer

(a) Optical Isomers of [Co(en)₃]³⁺:
The complex [Co(en)₃]³⁺ is an octahedral complex with non-superimposable mirror images (dextro and laevo forms).

![Diagram](Questions/images/Cyclic2-Q15.png)
The diagram shows the d- and l-enantiomers of octahedral [Co(en)₃]³⁺ displaying non-superimposable mirror symmetry.

(b) Reason for Optical Inactivity of trans-[CoCl₂(en)₂]⁺:
The trans-isomer of [CoCl₂(en)₂]⁺ possesses a plane of symmetry (and a center of inversion). As a result, its mirror image is identical and completely superimposable on the original molecule, rendering it achiral and optically inactive.
