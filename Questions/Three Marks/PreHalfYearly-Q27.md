Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

27. Write chemical equation in support of your answers for the following reactions:
(i) Anisole is treated with CH₃Cl / anhydrous AlCl₃?
(ii) Ethanol is heated at 443 K
(iii) (CH₃)₃C–OH is heated with Cu at 573 K?

## Answer

(i) Friedel-Crafts Alkylation of Anisole:
Anisole reacts with methyl chloride in the presence of anhydrous AlCl₃ to give 4-methoxytoluene as the major product:
C₆H₅–OCH₃(l) + CH₃Cl(l) --[anhydrous AlCl₃]--> 4-CH₃–C₆H₄–OCH₃(l) [major] + 2-CH₃–C₆H₄–OCH₃(l) [minor] + HCl(g)

(ii) Dehydration of Ethanol at 443 K:
Heating ethanol with concentrated H₂SO₄ at 443 K causes intramolecular dehydration to yield ethene:
CH₃–CH₂–OH(l) --[conc. H₂SO₄, 443 K]--> CH₂=CH₂(g)↑ + H₂O(l)

(iii) Dehydration of tert-Butyl Alcohol over Cu at 573 K:
Passing vapors of tertiary butyl alcohol over heated copper at 573 K causes elimination of water to form 2-methylpropene:
(CH₃)₃C–OH(g) --[Cu, 573 K]--> (CH₃)₂C=CH₂(g)↑ + H₂O(g)
