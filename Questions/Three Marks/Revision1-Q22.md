Chapter: Biomolecules

22. (A) What are reducing sugars? Give example.
(B) Write any one difference between fibrous and globular protein
(C) Mention the different types of RNA

## Answer

(a) Reducing Sugars:
Carbohydrates containing a free aldehyde or ketone group capable of reducing Fehling's solution and Tollens' reagent. Examples: Glucose, Fructose, Maltose, Lactose.

(b) Difference between Fibrous and Globular Proteins:
Fibrous proteins consist of parallel polypeptide chains held together by hydrogen and disulfide bonds forming insoluble fiber-like structures (e.g., keratin, collagen). Globular proteins fold into compact spherical shapes soluble in water (e.g., insulin, albumin).

(c) Types of RNA:
Messenger RNA (mRNA), Ribosomal RNA (rRNA), and Transfer RNA (tRNA).
