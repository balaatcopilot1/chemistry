Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

19. Write the structure of the major organic product in each of the following reactions:
(i) CH₃CH₂CH=CH₂ + HBr →
(ii) C₆H₅ONa + C₂H₅Cl →
(iii) CH₃CH(Br)CH₂CH₃ + NaOH →

## Answer

(i) 2-Bromobutane: CH₃–CH₂–CH(Br)–CH₃
(Electrophilic addition of HBr follows Markovnikov's rule, placing Br at the secondary carbon.)

(ii) Phenetole (Ethoxybenzene): C₆H₅–O–C₂H₅
(Williamson ether synthesis involving nucleophilic substitution of chloride by sodium phenoxide.)

(iii) Butan-2-ol: CH₃–CH(OH)–CH₂–CH₃
(Nucleophilic substitution of bromide by hydroxide ion in aqueous NaOH.)

*Source: NCERT, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*
