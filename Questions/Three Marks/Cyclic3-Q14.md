Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

14. Write chemical reaction to show the following transformations:
(i) Benzene to Acetophenone
(ii) Butan-1-ol to butanoic acid

## Answer

(i) Benzene to Acetophenone:
Benzene undergoes Friedel-Crafts acylation with acetyl chloride in the presence of anhydrous aluminium chloride catalyst to yield acetophenone.

Equation:
C₆H₆(l) + CH₃COCl(l) --[anhydrous AlCl₃]--> C₆H₅COCH₃(l) + HCl(g)

(ii) Butan-1-ol to butanoic acid:
Butan-1-ol is oxidized directly to butanoic acid by treatment with acidified potassium dichromate or alkaline potassium permanganate solution.

Equation:
CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂OH(l) + 2 [O] --[K₂Cr₂O⇈ / H₂SO₄, heat]--> CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH(l) + H₂O(l)
