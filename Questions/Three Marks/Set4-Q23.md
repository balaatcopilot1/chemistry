Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

23. What happens when :
(a) Propanenitrile is treated with phenyl magnesium bromide followed by hydrolysis ?
(b) p-fluorotoluene is treated with CrO₃ in the presence of acetic anhydride followed by hydrolysis with aqueous acid.
(c) Phthalic acid is treated with NH₃ followed by heating ?
Write chemical reactions in support of your answer.

## Answer

(a) Addition of Grignard reagent to propanenitrile followed by acidic hydrolysis yields 1-phenylpropan-1-one (propiophenone):
CH₃CH₂CN + C₆H₅MgBr --[ether]--> CH₃CH₂C(=NMgBr)C₆H₅ --[H₃O⁺]--> C₆H₅COCH₂CH₃ + Mg(OH)Br + NH₃

(b) Oxidation of p-fluorotoluene with CrO₃ in acetic anhydride forms a gem-diacetate intermediate which on hydrolysis with aqueous acid yields 4-fluorobenzaldehyde:
4-F–C₆H₄–CH₃ + CrO₃ + (CH₃CO)₂O → 4-F–C₆H₄–CH(OCOCH₃)₂ --[H₃O⁺, heat]--> 4-F–C₆H₄–CHO + 2 CH₃COOH

(c) Reaction of phthalic acid with ammonia forms ammonium phthalate, which on heating loses water to yield phthalamide, and on further strong heating loses NH₃ to form phthalimide:
C₆H₄(COOH)₂ + 2 NH₃ → C₆H₄(COONH₄)₂ --[heat, -2H₂O]--> C₆H₄(CONH₂)₂ --[strong heat, -NH₃]--> C₆H₄(CO)₂NH [Phthalimide]
