Chapter: Electrochemistry

17. Oxidation of ketones involves carbon-carbon bond cleavage. Name the products formed on oxidation of 2,5-dimethylhexane-3-one.

## Answer

2,5-Dimethylhexane-3-one has the structure:
(CH₃)₂CH–CH₂–CO–CH(CH₃)₂

Upon vigorous oxidation with strong oxidizing agents (like HNO₃ or KMnO₄/H₂SO₄), carbon-carbon bond cleavage occurs on either side of the carbonyl group according to Popoff's rule:

Cleavage Path A (C2–C3 bond cleavage):
Yields 2-methylpropanoic acid (isobutyric acid) and 3-methylbutanoic acid (isovaleric acid).
Products:
1. 2-Methylpropanoic acid: (CH₃)₂CH–COOH
2. 3-Methylbutanoic acid: (CH₃)₂CH–CH₂–COOH

Cleavage Path B (C3–C4 bond cleavage):
Yields 2-methylpropanoic acid and propanoic acid derivatives.

Main products: 2-Methylpropanoic acid [(CH₃)₂CH–COOH] and 3-Methylbutanoic acid [(CH₃)₂CH–CH₂–COOH].

*Source: NCERT, Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids*
