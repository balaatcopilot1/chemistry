Chapter: Electrochemistry

19. The cell in which the following reaction occurs:
2Fe³⁺(aq) + 2I⁻(aq) → 2Fe²⁺(aq) + I₂(s) has E°cell = 0.236 V at 298 K. Calculate the standard Gibbs energy and the log K of the cell reaction.

## Answer

Given:
E°cell = 0.236 V
Temperature, T = 298 K
Number of electrons involved, n = 2 (2Fe³⁺ + 2e⁻ → 2Fe²⁺)
Faraday's constant, F = 96,500 C mol⁻¹

Step 1: Calculate Standard Gibbs Energy (ΔrG°):
Formula: ΔrG° = -n F E°cell
Substitution:
ΔrG° = -2 × 96500 C mol⁻¹ × 0.236 V
ΔrG° = -45548 J mol⁻¹ = -45.55 kJ mol⁻¹

Step 2: Calculate Equilibrium Constant (log K):
Formula: E°cell = (0.0591 / n) log K
0.236 = (0.0591 / 2) log K
0.236 = 0.02955 log K
log K = 0.236 / 0.02955 = 7.986 (or ~ 7.99)

Answer:
Standard Gibbs Energy (ΔrG°) = -45.55 kJ mol⁻¹
log K = 7.986
