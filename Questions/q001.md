The decomposition of N₂O₅ is a first order reaction: 2N₂O₅(g) → 4NO₂(g) + O₂(g). At 318 K its half-life is 1386 s.

(a) Write the rate law for this reaction.
(b) Calculate the rate constant k.
(c) State the unit of k for a first order reaction.
(d) What fraction of N₂O₅ remains after 2 half-lives?

## Answer

**(a)** Rate = k [N₂O₅]

**(b)** For a first order reaction, k = 0.693 / t½ = 0.693 / 1386 s = 5.0 × 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹

**(c)** s⁻¹

**(d)** After 2 half-lives, the fraction left is (1/2) × (1/2) = 1/4.
