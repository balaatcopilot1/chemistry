29. Observe the following figure and answer the following questions:
![Diagram](Questions/images/Revision2-Q29.png)
The graph shows total vapour pressure and partial pressures higher than expected from Raoult's law as a function of mole fraction.

a) What is the deviation shown in the graph? Give one example. (1)
b) What type of intermolecular interaction is expected for such deviations? (1)
c) i) Define azeotropes. What type of azeotrope is formed by solutions showing the above deviation? (2)
OR
c) ii) State Raoult's law. Write 2 characteristics of a solution obeying Raoult's law. (2)

## Answer

(a) Positive deviation from Raoult's law. Example: Mixture of ethanol and acetone (or cyclohexane and ethanol).

(b) Intermolecular A–B attractive forces between solute and solvent molecules are weaker than A–A (solute-solute) and B–B (solvent-solvent) attractive forces in pure components.

(c) (i) Azeotropes are binary liquid mixtures having the same composition in liquid and vapour phase and boiling at a constant temperature. Solutions showing positive deviation form minimum boiling azeotropes at a specific composition.

OR

(c) (ii) Raoult's law states that for a solution of volatile liquids, the partial vapour pressure of each component is directly proportional to its mole fraction present in the solution (pA = pA° · xA).
Characteristics of ideal solutions obeying Raoult's law:
1. Enthalpy of mixing is zero (ΔmixH = 0).
2. Volume of mixing is zero (ΔmixV = 0).
