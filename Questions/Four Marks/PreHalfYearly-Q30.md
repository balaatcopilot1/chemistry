30. The substitution reaction of alkyl halide mainly occurs by SN1 or SN2 mechanism. Whatever mechanism alkyl halides follow for the substitution reaction to occur; the polarity of the carbon halogen bond is responsible for these substitution reactions. The rate of SN1 reactions are governed by the stability of carbocation whereas for SN2 reactions steric factor is the deciding factor. If the starting material is a chiral compound, we may end up with an inverted product or racemic mixture depending upon the type of mechanism followed by alkyl halide.

a) Which of the following is most reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction: CH₂=CHCl or CH₃CH=CHCl. Why?
b) What is a chiral carbon. Identify the chiral compound in the figure above.
c) Which pair undergoes SN1 reaction faster. Why?
d) i) Racemic mixture is optically inactive. Give reason. (1)
OR
d) ii) Aryl halides is less reactive towards nucleophilic substitution reaction. Give reason.

## Answer

(a) CH₃CH=CHCl is slightly more reactive than CH₂=CHCl, but both vinyl halides undergo nucleophilic substitution with extreme difficulty due to partial double bond character of C–Cl bond from resonance with the double bond.

(b) A chiral carbon (asymmetric carbon) is a tetrahedral carbon atom bonded to four different atoms or groups of atoms.

(c) For SN1 reactions, tertiary alkyl halides react faster than secondary and primary because SN1 rates are governed by carbocation stability (3° > 2° > 1°). Between (CH₃)₃C–Br and (CH₃)₂CH–Br, (CH₃)₃C–Br reacts faster as it forms a stable tertiary carbocation.

(d) (i) A racemic mixture contains equal proportions of (+) and (-) enantiomers; the dextrorotation of one isomer is exactly counterbalanced by the laevorotation of the other (external compensation), resulting in zero net optical rotation.

OR

(d) (ii) Aryl halides are unreactive towards nucleophilic substitution because:
1. Partial double bond character of C–Cl bond due to resonance with the benzene ring.
2. sp² hybridization of ring carbon holding halogen more tightly than sp³ carbon.
3. Instability of phenyl cation intermediate.
