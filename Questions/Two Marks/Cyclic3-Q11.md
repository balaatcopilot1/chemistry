Chapter: Electrochemistry

11. λm° for NaCl, HCl and NaAc are 126.4, 425.9 and 91.0 S cm² mol⁻¹ respectively. Calculate λm° for HAc.

## Answer

According to Kohlrausch's Law of independent migration of ions:
λm°(HAc) = λm°(H⁺) + λm°(Ac⁻)

Expressing in terms of given strong electrolytes:
λm°(HAc) = λm°(HCl) + λm°(NaAc) - λm°(NaCl)

Substitution:
λm°(HAc) = 425.9 + 91.0 - 126.4
λm°(HAc) = 516.9 - 126.4 = 390.5 S cm² mol⁻¹

Answer:
390.5 S cm² mol⁻¹
