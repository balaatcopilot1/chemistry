Chapter: Amines

21. Complete the following reaction:
i) Benzamide --[LiAlH₄ / H₂O]--> ?
ii) Benzamide + Br₂ + KOH → ?

## Answer

(i) Reduction with LiAlH₄:
Benzamide is reduced by lithium aluminium hydride followed by hydrolysis to yield benzylamine:
C₆H₅–CO–NH₂(s) --[(1) LiAlH₄ / ether, (2) H₂O]--> C₆H₅–CH₂–NH₂(l) + H₂O(l)
Product: Benzylamine (1-phenylmethanamine)

(ii) Hofmann Bromamide Degradation:
Benzamide reacts with bromine and ethanolic KOH to lose the carbonyl carbon and form aniline:
C₆H₅–CO–NH₂(s) + Br₂(l) + 4 KOH(aq) → C₆H₅–NH₂(l) + K₂CO₃(aq) + 2 KBr(aq) + 2 H₂O(l)
Product: Aniline (Benzenamine)
