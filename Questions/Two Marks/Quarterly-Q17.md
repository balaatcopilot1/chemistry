Chapter: Solutions

17. Write any two differences between Positive deviation and Negative deviation.

## Answer

(a) Intermolecular Forces:
- Positive Deviation: Solute-solvent (A–B) attractive interactions are weaker than pure solute-solute (A–A) and solvent-solvent (B–B) interactions.
- Negative Deviation: Solute-solvent (A–B) attractive interactions are stronger than pure solute-solute (A–A) and solvent-solvent (B–B) interactions.

(b) Enthalpy and Volume Changes of Mixing:
- Positive Deviation: ΔmixH > 0 (endothermic) and ΔmixV > 0 (expansion).
- Negative Deviation: ΔmixH < 0 (exothermic) and ΔmixV < 0 (contraction).
