Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

17. Answer the following questions on the basis of the given plot of potential energy vs Reaction coordinate:

(A) What is the threshold energy for the reaction?
(B) What is the activation energy for forward reaction?

![Diagram](Questions/images/Revision1-Q17.png)
The diagram shows a potential energy profile with reactant R at 150 kJ, activated complex C at 300 kJ, and product P at 50 kJ.

## Answer

(a) Threshold Energy:
Threshold energy = 300 kJ (Peak energy of the activated complex C).

(b) Activation Energy for Forward Reaction:
Activation energy (Eₐ) = Threshold energy - Energy of reactants (R)
Eₐ = 300 kJ - 150 kJ = 150 kJ.

*Source: NCERT, Chemical Kinetics*
