Chapter: Biomolecules

20. What are the hydrolysis products of the following ?
(a) Lactose
(b) DNA containing Thymine

## Answer

(a) Hydrolysis of lactose (a disaccharide) yields one molecule of β-D-galactose and one molecule of β-D-glucose:
C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ + H₂O --[lactase]--> β-D-galactose + β-D-glucose

(b) Complete hydrolysis of DNA containing thymine yields:
1. 2-deoxy-D-ribose sugar
2. Phosphoric acid (H₃PO₄)
3. Nitrogenous bases: Thymine (T), Adenine (A), Guanine (G), and Cytosine (C).

*Source: NCERT, Biomolecules*
