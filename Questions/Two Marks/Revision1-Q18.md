Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

18. Complete the following:
(A) Cr₂O₇²⁻ + H⁺ + I⁻ →
(B) Cr₂O₇²⁻ + H⁺ + Fe²⁺ →

## Answer

(a) Reaction with Iodide:
Cr₂O₇²⁻(aq) + 14 H⁺(aq) + 6 I⁻(aq) → 2 Cr³⁺(aq) + 3 I₂(s) + 7 H₂O(l)

(b) Reaction with Iron(II):
Cr₂O₇²⁻(aq) + 14 H⁺(aq) + 6 Fe²⁺(aq) → 2 Cr³⁺(aq) + 6 Fe³⁺(aq) + 7 H₂O(l)

*Source: NCERT, The d- and f-Block Elements*
