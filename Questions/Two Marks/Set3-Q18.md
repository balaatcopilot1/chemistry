Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

18. For a reaction A + B → Products, the rate law is :
Rate = k[A][B]³/²
Write the overall order of the reaction. Can this reaction be an elementary reaction ? Give reason in support of your answer.

## Answer

(a) Overall Order = 1 + 3/2 = 5/2 = 2.5 (Fractional order).
(b) No, this reaction cannot be an elementary reaction because elementary reactions proceed in a single step with integral molecularity (1, 2, or 3) and cannot have fractional orders.
