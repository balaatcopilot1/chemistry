Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

19. A. i) Write the products formed when Propanol reacts with thionyl chloride.
ii) Convert propene to 1-iodo propane.
OR
B. Identify A and B in the following transformation:
![Diagram](Questions/images/Revision2-Q19.png)
The reaction scheme shows propene reacting under specified conditions to yield intermediate A and final product B.

## Answer

A. (i) Propan-1-ol reacts with thionyl chloride (SOCl₂) to produce 1-chloropropane, sulfur dioxide gas, and hydrogen chloride gas:
CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–OH(l) + SOCl₂(l) → CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–Cl(l) + SO₂(g)↑ + HCl(g)↑

(ii) Convert propene to 1-iodopropane:
Step 1: Treat propene with HBr in the presence of benzoyl peroxide (anti-Markovnikov addition) to obtain 1-bromopropane:
CH₃–CH=CH₂(g) + HBr(g) --[peroxide]--> CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–Br(l)
Step 2: React 1-bromopropane with NaI in dry acetone (Finkelstein reaction) to yield 1-iodopropane:
CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–Br(l) + NaI(aq) --[dry acetone]--> CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–I(l) + NaBr(s)↓

OR

B. Transformation identification:
Compound A: 1-Bromopropane [CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–Br] (formed by anti-Markovnikov addition of HBr in peroxide)
Compound B: 1-Iodopropane [CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–I] (formed by halogen exchange with NaI in acetone)
