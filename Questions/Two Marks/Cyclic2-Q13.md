Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

13. Write the chemical reaction for the preparation of propan-1-ol from propene using the hydroboration-oxidation method.

## Answer

Propene reacts with diborane (B₂H₆) to give tripropylborane, which is subsequently oxidized by hydrogen peroxide in the presence of aqueous sodium hydroxide to yield propan-1-ol.

Step-wise Equations:

1. Hydroboration Step:
3 CH₃–CH=CH₂(g) + 1/2 B₂H₆(g) → (CH₃–CH₂–CH₂)₃B(l)

2. Oxidation Step:
(CH₃–CH₂–CH₂)₃B(l) + 3 H₂O₂(aq) --[NaOH(aq)]--> 3 CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–OH(aq) + H₃BO₃(aq)

Overall Reaction:
CH₃–CH=CH₂(g) + H₂O(l) --[1. B₂H₆, 2. H₂O₂/OH⁻]--> CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–OH(aq)

This addition follows anti-Markovnikov rule.
