Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

17. For the compound having molecular formula C₄H₉Br, write :
1. the isomer which is most reactive towards SN1 displacement.
2. the isomer which, on reacting with Na metal in the presence of dry ether, gives 2,5-Dimethylhexane.

## Answer

(a) 2-Bromo-2-methylpropane [(CH₃)₃C–Br] (tertiary alkyl halide forms stable 3° carbocation).
(b) 1-Bromo-2-methylpropane [(CH₃)₂CH–CH₂–Br] (isobutyl bromide reacts with Na in dry ether via Wurtz reaction to give 2,5-dimethylhexane).
