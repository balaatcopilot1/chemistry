Chapter: Coordination Compounds

10. Differentiate between a homoleptic and a heteroleptic complex using an example for each.

## Answer

(a) Homoleptic Complex:
A coordination complex in which the central metal ion or atom is bound to only one type of donor group (ligand).
Example: [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ or [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻

(b) Heteroleptic Complex:
A coordination complex in which the central metal ion or atom is bound to more than one type of donor group (ligand).
Example: [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺ or [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂]
