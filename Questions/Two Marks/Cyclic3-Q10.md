Chapter: Electrochemistry

10. How much charge is required for the conversion of 1 mol of Al³⁺ to Al?

## Answer

Step 1: Write the reduction half-reaction for Al³⁺ ion:
Al³⁺(aq) + 3e⁻ → Al(s)

Step 2: Determine the moles of electrons required:
Reduction of 1 mole of Al³⁺ requires 3 moles of electrons.

Step 3: Calculate the charge in Coulombs:
Charge (Q) = n × F
Q = 3 × 96,500 C mol⁻¹ = 289,500 C (or 3 Faraday)

Answer:
2.895 × 10⁵ C (or 3 F)
