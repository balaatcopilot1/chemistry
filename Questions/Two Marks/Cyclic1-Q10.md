Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

10. Chloroform is stored in closed, dark-coloured bottles that are completely filled. Give reason.

## Answer

Chloroform (CHCl₃) is slowly oxidized by oxygen in the presence of light to form carbonyl chloride (phosgene, COCl₂), an extremely poisonous gas.

Balanced Equation:
2 CHCl₃(l) + O₂(g) --[light]--> 2 COCl₂(g) + 2 HCl(g)

Storing chloroform in dark-coloured bottles filled to the brim excludes air and light, preventing this hazardous degradation reaction.

*Source: NCERT, Haloalkanes and Haloarenes*
