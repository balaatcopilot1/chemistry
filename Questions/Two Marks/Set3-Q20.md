Chapter: Coordination Compounds

20. (a) Write IUPAC names of the following compounds :
1. [PtCl₂(en)₂]SO₄
2. (NH₄)₂[CoF₄]
OR
(b) Define the following terms with a suitable example in each case :
1. Ambidentate ligand
2. Double salt

## Answer

(a) 1. Dichloridobis(ethane-1,2-diamine)platinum(IV) sulfate
2. Ammonium tetrafluoridocobaltate(II)
OR
(b) 1. Ambidentate ligand: A unidentate ligand that can bind to the central metal atom/ion through two different donor atoms (e.g. NO₂⁻ binding via N as nitrito-N or O as nitrito-O; SCN⁻ binding via S or N).
2. Double salt: An addition compound that exists only in solid state and completely dissociates into its constituent simple ions when dissolved in water (e.g. Mohr's salt FeSO₄·(NH₄)₂SO₄·6H₂O).
