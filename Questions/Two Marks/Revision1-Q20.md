Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

20. (A) Calculate the 'spin only' magnetic moment of M²⁺(aq) ion (Z = 27).
(B) Actinoid contraction is greater from element to element than lanthanoid contraction. Why?

## Answer

(a) Spin-only Magnetic Moment:
For Z = 27 (Cobalt, Co), Co²⁺ has electronic configuration [Ar] 3d⁷, containing 3 unpaired electrons (n = 3).
Formula: μ = √(n(n+2))
Substitution: μ = √(3 × (3 + 2)) = √15 = 3.87 BM

(b) Actinoid Contraction Reason:
5f electrons in actinoids have poorer shielding efficiency against nuclear charge compared to 4f electrons in lanthanoids due to their more diffuse spatial distribution.
