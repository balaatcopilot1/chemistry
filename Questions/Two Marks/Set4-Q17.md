Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

17. (a) Reactions of which order will show the rate to be independent of the concentration of the reactant ? Give one example of this order.
(b) State the condition under which a bimolecular reaction may be kinetically a first order reaction.

## Answer

(a) Zero-order reactions show a reaction rate that is completely independent of the reactant concentration (Rate = k [A]⁰ = k).
Example: Thermal decomposition of gaseous ammonia on a hot platinum surface at high pressure:
2 NH₃(g) --[Pt catalyst, heat]--> N₂(g) + 3 H₂(g)

(b) A bimolecular reaction behaves as a pseudo first-order reaction when one of the reacting components is present in large excess compared to the other. Under this condition, the concentration of the excess reactant remains virtually constant throughout the reaction.
Example: Acid hydrolysis of ethyl acetate where water is in large excess:
CH₃COOC₂H₅ + H₂O --[H⁺]--> CH₃COOH + C₂H₅OH (Rate = k [CH₃COOC₂H₅]).
