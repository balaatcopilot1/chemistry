Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

21. Write structure of the products of the following reactions.
i) 4-nitrotoluene --[1. CrO₂Cl₂, 2. H₃O⁺]-->
ii) H₃CC≡CH --[Hg²⁺, H₂SO₄]-->

## Answer

(i) Product: 4-Nitrobenzaldehyde
Structure: O₂N–C₆H₄–CHO (para-nitrobenzaldehyde)
Explanation: Etard reaction oxidizes the methyl group of 4-nitrotoluene to an aldehyde group using chromyl chloride (CrO₂Cl₂) in CS₂ followed by acidic hydrolysis.

(ii) Product: Propanone (Acetone)
Structure: CH₃–CO–CH₃
Explanation: Hydration of propyne (H₃C–C≡CH) catalyzed by Hg²⁺ and H₂SO₄ adds water according to Markovnikov's rule to form an enol intermediate [CH₃–C(OH)=CH₂], which tautomerizes to propanone.
