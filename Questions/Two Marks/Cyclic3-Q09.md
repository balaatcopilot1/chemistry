Chapter: Electrochemistry

9. State Faraday's second law of electrolysis.

## Answer

Faraday's Second Law of Electrolysis states that when the same quantity of electricity is passed through different electrolytic solutions connected in series, the masses of different substances liberated or deposited at the electrodes are directly proportional to their chemical equivalent masses (or equivalent weights).

Formula:
w₁ / w₂ = E₁ / E₂

Where w₁ and w₂ are the masses of substances deposited, and E₁ and E₂ are their respective chemical equivalent masses.
