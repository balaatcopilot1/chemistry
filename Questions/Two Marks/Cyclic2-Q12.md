Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

12. What is denatured alcohol? Why is it produced commercially?

## Answer

(a) Denatured alcohol is ethanol that has been rendered unfit for human consumption by mixing it with toxic or foul-smelling substances such as methanol, pyridine, or copper sulphate (which imparts a distinctive blue colour).

(b) It is produced commercially to supply ethanol for industrial uses tax-free without the risk of it being diverted or misused as an alcoholic beverage.
