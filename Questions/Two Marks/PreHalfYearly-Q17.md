17. Calculate the temperature at which a solution containing 54 g of glucose, (C₆H₁₂O₆) in 250 g of water will freeze (Kf for water 1.86 K kg mol⁻¹).

## Answer

(a) Moles of glucose = Mass / Molar mass = 54 g / 180 g mol⁻¹ = 0.3 mol
(b) Molality of solution (m) = Moles of solute / Mass of solvent in kg = 0.3 mol / 0.25 kg = 1.2 mol kg⁻¹
(c) Depression in freezing point ΔTf = Kf × m = 1.86 K kg mol⁻¹ × 1.2 mol kg⁻¹ = 2.232 K (or 2.232 °C)
(d) Freezing point of solution = Tf° - ΔTf = 0 °C - 2.232 °C = -2.232 °C (or 270.918 K).
