Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

19. i) p-dichlorobenzene has a higher melting point than those of o- and m- isomers. Why?
ii) Explain the Wurtz-fittig reaction.

## Answer

(i) p-Dichlorobenzene possesses a symmetrical para-structure that fits more compactly and tightly into the crystal lattice compared to the less symmetrical ortho- and meta-isomers. Consequently, more thermal energy is required to break the crystal lattice, giving it a higher melting point.

(ii) Wurtz-Fittig Reaction:
When a mixture of an aryl halide and an alkyl halide is treated with sodium metal in dry ether, an alkylbenzene is formed.

Equation:
C₆H₅–Cl(l) + 2 Na(s) + Cl–CH₃(l) --[dry ether]--> C₆H₅–CH₃(l) + 2 NaCl(s)
