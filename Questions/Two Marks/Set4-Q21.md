Chapter: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

21. Draw the structures of major product(s) in each of the following reactions :
(a) 1-chloro-2,4-dinitrobenzene + (i) NaOH, 368 K (ii) H⁺ → ?
![Diagram](Questions/images/Set4-Q21.png)
The diagram shows 1-chloro-2,4-dinitrobenzene reacting with aqueous NaOH at 368 K followed by acidification.
(b) CH₃CH = C(CH₃)₂ + HBr → ?

## Answer

(a) Nucleophilic aromatic substitution of chloride by hydroxyl group yields 2,4-dinitrophenol:
C₆H₃Cl(NO₂)₂ + NaOH --[368 K; H⁺]--> C₆H₃(OH)(NO₂)₂ [2,4-Dinitrophenol] + NaCl

(b) Electrophilic addition of HBr according to Markovnikov's rule yields 2-bromo-2-methylbutane:
CH₃–CH=C(CH₃)₂ + HBr → CH₃–CH₂–C(Br)(CH₃)₂ [2-Bromo-2-methylbutane]

![Diagram](Questions/images/Set4-A21.png)
The diagram shows the major products: 2,4-dinitrophenol for reaction (a) and 2-bromo-2-methylbutane for reaction (b).
