Chapter: Amines

21. Give one chemical test to distinguish between the following pair of compounds:
(A) Methylamine and dimethylamine
(B) Give Reason: pKb of aniline is more than that of methylamine

## Answer

(a) Carbylamine Test:
Warming methylamine (1° amine) with chloroform and ethanolic KOH produces a foul-smelling methyl isocyanide precipitate/gas:
CH₃NH₂ + CHCl₃ + 3 KOH(alc) --[heat]--> CH₃NC + 3 KCl + 3 H₂O
Dimethylamine (2° amine) does not undergo this reaction.

(b) Reason for pKb Difference:
In aniline, the unshared electron pair on nitrogen is delocalized into the aromatic ring via resonance, making it less available for protonation. In methylamine, the electron-donating +I effect of the methyl group increases electron density on nitrogen, making methylamine a stronger base (lower pKb) than aniline (higher pKb).
