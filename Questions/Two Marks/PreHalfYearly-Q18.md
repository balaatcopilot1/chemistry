Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

18. Distinguish between order and molecularity of a reaction.

## Answer

(a) Definition & Origin:
- Order: Sum of powers of concentration terms in the experimentally determined rate law.
- Molecularity: Total number of reacting species colliding simultaneously in an elementary reaction.

(b) Key Characteristics:
- Order: Can be zero, fractional, or negative, and is applicable to both elementary and complex reactions.
- Molecularity: Must be a positive integer (1, 2, 3), cannot be zero or fractional, and applies only to elementary steps.
