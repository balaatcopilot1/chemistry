Chapter: Solutions

18. (a) Explain the following with the help of Henry’s law :
(i) Bends (ii) Anoxia
OR
(b) How does sprinkling of salt help in clearing the snow covered roads in hilly areas ? Write the name of the colligative property involved in this process.

## Answer

(a) (i) Bends: Scuba divers breathe air at high pressure underwater, increasing N₂ solubility in blood. Upon returning to the surface, pressure drops and dissolved N₂ released as bubbles in capillaries, causing painful and dangerous medical condition called bends.
(ii) Anoxia: At high altitudes, low atmospheric partial pressure of O₂ leads to lower oxygen concentration in blood and tissues, causing climbers to feel weak and unable to think clearly (anoxia).

OR

(b) Sprinkling salt (NaCl or CaCl₂) on snow-covered roads lowers the freezing point of water below 0 ºC, causing the snow to melt at ambient temperatures and clearing the roads.
Colligative property involved: Depression of freezing point (ΔTf = i × Kf × m).
