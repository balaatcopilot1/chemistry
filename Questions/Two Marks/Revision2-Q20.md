Chapter: Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

20. i) Distinguish between Ethanol and Phenol.
ii) What is the oxidation product of phenol?

## Answer

(i) Chemical Distinction: Neutral FeCl₃ Test
Add neutral ferric chloride solution to both test tubes. Phenol reacts to give a characteristic violet-coloured complex solution, whereas ethanol does not produce any color change.
Equation: 6 C₆H₅OH(aq) + FeCl₃(aq) → [Fe(OC₆H₅)₆]³⁻(aq) + 3 H⁺(aq) + 3 HCl(aq) [violet complex]

(ii) Oxidation of Phenol:
Oxidation of phenol with chromic acid (Na₂Cr₂O⇇ / H₂SO₄) yields a conjugated diketone called 1,4-benzoquinone (p-benzoquinone).
Equation: C₆H₅OH(l) + [O] --[Na₂Cr₂O⇇ / H₂SO₄]--> O=C₆H₄=O(s) [1,4-benzoquinone] + H₂O(l)
