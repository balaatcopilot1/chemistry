Chapter: Biomolecules

21. How do you explain the following ?
1. Presence of a straight chain in glucose
2. Presence of five − OH groups in glucose which are attached to different carbon atoms

![Diagram](Questions/images/Set3-Q21.png)
The diagram shows chemical equations testing the open chain structure and hydroxyl content of D-glucose.

## Answer

(a) Prolonged heating of glucose with HI yields n-hexane, proving that all six carbon atoms in glucose form an unbranched straight chain:
C₆H₁₂O₆ --[HI, heat]--> CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃
(b) Acetylation of glucose with acetic anhydride yields glucose pentaacetate, confirming the presence of five hydroxyl (–OH) groups; since glucose is a stable compound, each –OH group must be attached to a different carbon atom.
