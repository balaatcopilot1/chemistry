Chapter: Biomolecules

19. (a) (A) Write a reaction which shows that all the carbon atoms in glucose are linked in a straight chain.
(B) Write the structure of the product obtained when glucose is oxidised with nitric acid.
(OR)
(b) (A) Name the types of intermolecular forces that stabilize 2° and 3° structures of proteins.
(B) Where are the fat soluble vitamins stored in our body?

## Answer

(a) (A) Prolonged heating of glucose with HI yields n-hexane, proving that all six carbon atoms form an unbranched straight chain:
C₆H₁₂O₆(s) --[HI, heat]--> CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃(l)

(B) Oxidation of glucose with concentrated nitric acid (HNO₃) yields saccharic acid:
HOOC–(CHOH)₄–COOH

(OR)

(b) (A) Hydrogen bonds, disulfide linkages, van der Waals forces, and electrostatic (salt bridge) attractions.

(B) Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, and K) are stored in the liver and adipose (fat-storing) tissues.

*Source: NCERT, Biomolecules*
