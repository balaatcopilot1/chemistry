Chapter: Coordination Compounds

11. A coordination compound has the formula CrCl₃·6H₂O. One mole of this compound, when treated with excess silver nitrate, precipitates two moles of silver chloride. Write the structural formula of the complex and its IUPAC name.

## Answer

(a) Structural Formula:
Since 1 mole of the compound yields 2 moles of AgCl precipitate upon reaction with excess AgNO₃, 2 chloride ions must reside outside the coordination sphere as ionizable counter-ions.
Structural Formula: [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂·H₂O

(b) IUPAC Name:
Pentaaquachloridochromium(III) chloride monohydrate
