Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

13. i) What happens when Ethanal reacts with excess of ethanol and acid?
ii) Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their boiling points:
CH₃CHO, CH₃CH₂OH, CH₃OCH₃, CH₃CH₂CH₃

## Answer

(i) When ethanal reacts with two equivalents of ethanol in the presence of dry HCl gas (acid catalyst), it forms a gem-dialkoxy compound called ethanal diethyl acetal (acetal).

Equation:
CH₃CHO(l) + 2 CH₃CH₂OH(l) --[dry HCl(g)]--> CH₃CH(OCH₂CH₃)₂(l) + H₂O(l)

(ii) Increasing order of boiling points:
CH₃CH₂CH₃ < CH₃OCH₃ < CH₃CHO < CH₃CH₂OH

Reason: Propane (alkane) has weak van der Waals forces, methoxymethane (ether) has weak dipole moment, ethanal (aldehyde) has stronger dipole-dipole interactions, and ethanol (alcohol) forms strong intermolecular hydrogen bonds.
