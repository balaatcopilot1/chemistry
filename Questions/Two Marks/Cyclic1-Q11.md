11. Calculate the molarity of a solution containing 4 g of NaOH in 500 mL solution.

## Answer

Given:
Mass of solute (NaOH), w = 4 g
Molar mass of NaOH, M = 23 + 16 + 1 = 40 g mol⁻¹
Volume of solution, V = 500 mL = 0.5 L

Formula:
Molarity (M) = Moles of solute / Volume of solution in L
Moles of NaOH = w / M = 4 / 40 = 0.1 mol

Substitution:
Molarity (M) = 0.1 / 0.5 = 0.2 mol L⁻¹

Answer:
0.2 M (or 0.2 mol L⁻¹)
