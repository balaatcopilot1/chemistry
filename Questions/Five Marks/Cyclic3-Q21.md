Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

21. (i) An alkene 'A' (Mol. formula C₅H₁₀) on ozonolysis gives a mixture of two compounds, 'B' and 'C'. Compound B' gives positive Fehling's test and forms iodoform on treatment with I₂ and NaOH. Compound C' does not give Fehling's test but forms iodoform. Identify the compounds A, B, and C. Write the reaction for ozonolysis and formation of iodoform from B and C. (2 ½)
ii) Write the structures of A to E (2 ½)
C₆H₆ --[CH₃COCl / Anhyd. AlCl₃]--> A --[Zn-Hg / conc. HCl]--> B --[(i) KMnO₄-KOH, Δ (ii) H₃O⁺]--> C
A --[NaOI]--> D + E

## Answer

(i) Part 1:
- Compound B gives Fehling's test and iodoform test, so B must be an aldehyde containing a CH₃C=O group, which is Ethanal (CH₃CHO).
- Compound C does not give Fehling's test but gives iodoform test, so C must be a ketone containing a CH₃C=O group, which is Propanone (CH₃COCH₃).
- Alkene A on ozonolysis yields ethanal and propanone, so A is 2-Methylbut-2-ene [(CH₃)₂C=CHCH₃].

Ozonolysis Equation:
(CH₃)₂C=CHCH₃ + O₃ --[Zn / H₂O]--> CH₃CHO + CH₃COCH₃

Iodoform Reactions:
From B (Ethanal):
CH₃CHO + 3 I₂ + 4 NaOH → CHI₃(s) [yellow ppt] + HCOONa + 3 NaI + 3 H₂O
From C (Propanone):
CH₃COCH₃ + 3 I₂ + 4 NaOH → CHI₃(s) [yellow ppt] + CH₃COONa + 3 NaI + 3 H₂O

Identities:
A = 2-Methylbut-2-ene [(CH₃)₂C=CHCH₃]
B = Ethanal [CH₃CHO]
C = Propanone [CH₃COCH₃]

(ii) Part 2: Structures of A to E:
- A: Acetophenone [C₆H₅COCH₃] (from Friedel-Crafts acylation of benzene)
- B: Ethylbenzene [C₆H₅CH₂CH₃] (from Clemmensen reduction of acetophenone)
- C: Benzoic acid [C₆H₅COOH] (from side-chain oxidation of ethylbenzene with alkaline KMnO₄)
- D: Iodoform [CHI₃] (from iodoform reaction of acetophenone with NaOI)
- E: Sodium benzoate [C₆H₅COONa] (from iodoform reaction of acetophenone with NaOI)

Structures:
A = C₆H₅–CO–CH₃
B = C₆H₅–CH₂–CH₃
C = C₆H₅–COOH
D = CHI₃
E = C₆H₅–COONa

*Source: NCERT, Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids*
