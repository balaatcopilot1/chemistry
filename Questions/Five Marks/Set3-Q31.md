Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

31. (a) (i) Describe giving reason which one of the following pairs has the property indicated : [3 Marks]
1. Fe or Cu − higher melting point
2. Ti³⁺ or Sc³⁺ − coloured in aqueous solution
3. Cr or Zn − higher third ionisation enthalpy
(ii) Write the ionic equations for the oxidizing action of MnO₄– in acidic medium with : [2 Marks]
1. Fe²⁺ ion
2. I⁻ ion
OR
(b) (i) A black-brown coloured solid (A) when fused with KOH in the presence of air, produces a dark green coloured compound (B) which on electrolytic oxidation in alkaline medium gives a dark purple coloured compound (C). Identify (A), (B) and (C). Write the reactions involved. [3 Marks]
(ii) What happens when an acidic solution of the green compound (B) is allowed to stand for some time ? Also write the equation involved. What is this type of reaction called ? [2 Marks]

## Answer

(a) (i) 1. Fe has a higher melting point than Cu because Fe (3d⁶ 4s²) has 4 unpaired d-electrons forming strong interatomic metallic bonds, whereas Cu (3d¹⁰ 4s¹) has filled d-orbitals with weaker bonding.
2. Ti³⁺ (3d¹) is coloured in aqueous solution due to d-d transition of its single unpaired d-electron, whereas Sc³⁺ (3d⁰) has an empty d-subshell and is colourless.
3. Zn has a higher third ionisation enthalpy than Cr because removing a 3rd electron from Zn²⁺ (3d¹⁰) requires breaking a stable, completely filled d¹⁰ subshell, whereas for Cr²⁺ (3d⁴) it yields a stable half-filled t2g³ (3d³) configuration.
(ii) 1. MnO₄⁻(aq) + 5 Fe²⁺(aq) + 8 H⁺(aq) → Mn²⁺(aq) + 5 Fe³⁺(aq) + 4 H₂O(l)
2. 2 MnO₄⁻(aq) + 10 I⁻(aq) + 16 H⁺(aq) → 2 Mn²⁺(aq) + 5 I₂(s) + 8 H₂O(l)
OR
(b) (i) Identification:
- Solid (A) = Manganese dioxide [MnO₂] (black-brown)
- Compound (B) = Potassium manganate [K₂MnO₄] (dark green)
- Compound (C) = Potassium permanganate [KMnO₄] (dark purple)
Reactions:
Fusion: 2 MnO₂ + 4 KOH + O₂ --[heat]--> 2 K₂MnO₄ + 2 H₂O
Electrolytic oxidation: MnO₄²⁻ --[oxidation at anode]--> MnO₄⁻ + e⁻
(ii) Manganate ion (B) in acidic medium disproportionates into purple permanganate (C) and brown manganese dioxide (A):
3 MnO₄²⁻(aq) + 4 H⁺(aq) → 2 MnO₄⁻(aq) + MnO₂(s)↓ + 2 H₂O(l)
This reaction is called a disproportionation reaction.
