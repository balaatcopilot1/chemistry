Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

32. (a) (i) Write the product(s) when : [3 Marks]
1. One mol of ethanal is treated with 1 mol of CH₃OH in the presence of dry HCl gas.
2. Benzaldehyde is treated with conc. NaOH.
3. Ethanoic acid is heated in the presence of P₂O₅.
(ii) Write a simple chemical test to distinguish between Ethanal and Propanal. [1 Mark]
(iii) Write the name of the reagent to transform Allyl alcohol to Propenal. [1 Mark]
OR
(b) (i) Draw the structure of the semicarbazone of acetone. [1 Mark]
(ii) Why are α-hydrogen atoms of aldehydes and ketones acidic in nature ? [1 Mark]
(iii) Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their reactivity towards HCN :
CH₃COCH₃, CH₃CHO, (CH₃)₃C − C(=O) − CH₃ [1 Mark]
(iv) Write the reaction involved in Etard reaction. [1 Mark]
(v) Write the product when cyclohexanecarbaldehyde reacts with Zn(Hg)/conc. HCl. [1 Mark]

## Answer

(a) (i) 1. Ethanal reacts with 1 mol CH₃OH in dry HCl gas to form 1-methoxyethanol (hemiacetal):
CH₃CHO + CH₃OH --[dry HCl]--> CH₃CH(OH)(OCH₃)
2. Benzaldehyde undergoes Cannizzaro reaction with conc. NaOH to yield benzyl alcohol and sodium benzoate:
2 C₆H₅CHO + NaOH → C₆H₅CH₂OH + C₆H₅COONa
3. Ethanoic acid on heating with P₂O₅ undergoes dehydration to form ethanoic anhydride:
2 CH₃COOH --[P₂O₅, heat]--> (CH₃CO)₂O + H₂O
(ii) Iodoform Test: Ethanal (CH₃CHO) reacts with I₂ and NaOH on warming to form a yellow precipitate of iodoform (CHI₃), whereas propanal (CH₃CH₂CHO) does not.
(iii) Pyridinium chlorochromate (PCC) or MnO₂.
OR
(b) (i) Structure of acetone semicarbazone: (CH₃)₂C=N–NH–CONH₂.
(ii) The strong electron-withdrawing inductive effect of the carbonyl group (>C=O) and the resonance stabilization of the enolate anion formed after proton loss make α-hydrogens acidic.
(iii) Reactivity towards HCN: (CH₃)₃C–CO–CH₃ < CH₃COCH₃ < CH₃CHO
(iv) Etard Reaction: Toluene is oxidized by chromyl chloride (CrO₂Cl₂) in CS₂ to form a chromium complex, which on hydrolysis yields benzaldehyde:
C₆H₅CH₃ + 2 CrO₂Cl₂ --[CS₂]--> C₆H₅CH(OCrOHCl₂)₂ --[H₃O⁺]--> C₆H₅CHO + 2 Cr(OH)₂Cl₂
(v) Clemmensen reduction converts cyclohexanecarbaldehyde to methylcyclohexane:
C₆H₁₁CHO + 4 [H] --[Zn(Hg) / conc. HCl]--> C₆H₁₁CH₃ (Methylcyclohexane) + H₂O
