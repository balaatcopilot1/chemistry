Describe the preparation of potassium dichromate (K₂Cr₂O₇) from chromite ore, with equations.

## Answer

1. Fusion with sodium carbonate in excess air:
   4FeCr₂O₄ + 8Na₂CO₃ + 7O₂ → 8Na₂CrO₄ + 2Fe₂O₃ + 8CO₂
2. Acidification of sodium chromate:
   2Na₂CrO₄ + 2H⁺ → Na₂Cr₂O₇ + 2Na⁺ + H₂O
3. Treatment with KCl:
   Na₂Cr₂O₇ + 2KCl → K₂Cr₂O₇ + 2NaCl
