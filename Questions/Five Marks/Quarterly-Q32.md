Chapter: Coordination Compounds

32. (a) A metal ion Mⁿ⁺ having d⁴ valence electronic configuration combines with three bidentate ligands to form a complex compound. Assuming Δ⒪ > P:
(i) Explain orbital splitting during this complex formation.
(ii) Write the electronic configuration of the valence electrons of the metal Mⁿ⁺ ion in terms of t₂g and eg.
(iii) What type of hybridisation will Mⁿ⁺ ion have?
(iv) Name the type of isomerism exhibited by this complex.
(v) Name a complex used in treatment of cancer.
(OR)
(b) Using valence bond theory explain the [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ in relation to the terms given below:
(i) Type of hybridisation
(ii) Inner or outer orbital complex
(iii) Magnetic behaviour
(iv) Spin only magnetic moment value.
(v) What is meant by the denticity of a ligand?

## Answer

(a) (i) Orbital Splitting: In an octahedral crystal field, the 5 d-orbitals split into two sets: lower energy t₂g (dxy, dyz, dzx) and higher energy eg (dx²-y², dz²). Since Δ⒪ > P (strong field case), electrons pair up in the lower t₂g set before populating eg.
(ii) Electronic Configuration: t₂g⁴ eg⁰
(iii) Hybridization: d²sp³ (inner orbital octahedral)
(iv) Isomerism: Optical isomerism (enantiomerism due to non-superimposable d- and l-isomers of [M(LL)₃]ⁿ⁺).
(v) Cancer treatment complex: Cisplatin, cis-[Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂].

(OR)

(b) For [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ (Co³⁺ is 3d⁶):
(i) Type of Hybridisation: d²sp³
(ii) Inner or Outer Orbital Complex: Inner orbital complex (utilizes 3d orbitals for hybridization).
(iii) Magnetic Behaviour: Diamagnetic (all 6 d-electrons are paired in 3d).
(iv) Spin-only Magnetic Moment: μ = √(n(n+2)) = √(0) = 0 BM.
(v) Denticity of a Ligand: The number of donor atoms through which a single ligand binds to the central metal ion in a coordination complex.
