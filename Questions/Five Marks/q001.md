(a) State Kohlrausch’s law of independent migration of ions.
(b) Write the relation between conductivity (κ) and molar conductivity (Λm).
(c) Write the Nernst equation for the half-cell reaction Cu²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻ → Cu(s).

## Answer

**(a)** At infinite dilution, the limiting molar conductivity of an electrolyte equals the sum of the limiting molar conductivities of its cations and anions.

**(b)** Λm = κ × 1000 / c (κ in S cm⁻¹, c in mol L⁻¹, Λm in S cm² mol⁻¹)

**(c)** E(Cu²⁺/Cu) = E°(Cu²⁺/Cu) − (0.059/2) log (1/[Cu²⁺]) at 298 K
