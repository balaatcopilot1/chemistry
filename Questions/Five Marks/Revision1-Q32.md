32. (a) (A) For the hydrolysis of methyl acetate in aqueous solution, the following results were obtained:
t/s: 0, 30, 60
[CH₃COOCH₃]/mol L⁻¹: 0.60, 0.30, 0.15
Show that it follows pseudo first order reaction, as the concentration of water remains constant.
(B) Calculate the average rate of reaction between the time interval 30 to 60 seconds. (Given log 2 = 0.3010, log 4 = 0.6021)
(OR)
(b) (A) For a certain chemical reaction variation of ln[R] vs. time (s) plot is given below:
![Diagram](Questions/images/Revision1-Q32.png)
The diagram shows a plot of ln[R] versus time with a negative slope equal to -k and y-intercept equal to ln[R]₀.
(i) Predict the order of the given reaction.
(ii) What does the slope of the line and intercept indicate?
(iii) Give an example of first order reaction.
(B) A first order reaction takes 160 minutes time for 20% completion. Calculate time required for half completion of reaction. (log 1.25 = 0.0969, log 5 = 0.6989)

## Answer

(a) (A) Pseudo First-Order Verification:
Formula: k = (2.303 / t) log ([R]₀ / [R]t)
At t = 30 s: k₁ = (2.303 / 30) log (0.60 / 0.30) = (2.303 × 0.3010) / 30 = 0.0231 s⁻¹
At t = 60 s: k₂ = (2.303 / 60) log (0.60 / 0.15) = (2.303 × 0.6021) / 60 = 0.0231 s⁻¹
Since k is constant, the reaction is pseudo first-order.

(B) Average Rate (30 s to 60 s):
Average Rate = - (0.15 - 0.30) / (60 - 30) = 0.15 / 30 = 0.005 mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹

(OR)

(b) (A) (i) First-order reaction.
(ii) Slope = -k (rate constant), Intercept = ln[R]₀.
(iii) Decomposition of N₂O₅: 2 N₂O₅(g) → 4 NO₂(g) + O₂(g).

(B) Half-Life Calculation:
k = (2.303 / 160) log (1 / 0.8) = (2.303 / 160) log 1.25 = (2.303 × 0.0969) / 160 = 0.001394 min⁻¹
t₁/₂ = 0.693 / k = 0.693 / 0.001394 = 496.8 min
