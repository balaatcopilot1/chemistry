Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

31. (a) (A) When MnO₂ is fused with KOH in the presence of KNO₃ as an oxidizing agent, it gives a dark green compound (A). Compound (A) disproportionates in an acidic solution to give purple compound (B). An alkaline solution of compound (B) oxidises KI to compound (C) whereas an acidified solution of compound (B) oxidises KI to (D). Identify (A), (B), (C), and (D) and explain with balanced chemical equations.
(B) What is meant by 'lanthanoid contraction'?
(OR)
(b) Give reasons :
(A) Zirconium (Z = 40) and Hafnium (Z = 72) have almost similar atomic radii.
(B) How d-block elements exhibit more oxidation states ?
(C) Why iron has high enthalpy of atomization ?
(D) Why Orange solution of potassium dichromate turns yellow on adding sodium hydroxide to it.

## Answer

(a) (A) Identification:
- Compound A = Potassium manganate, K₂MnO₄ (dark green)
- Compound B = Potassium permanganate, KMnO₄ (purple)
- Compound C = Potassium iodate, KIO₃
- Compound D = Iodine, I₂

Balanced Chemical Equations:
1. Fusion of MnO₂:
2 MnO₂(s) + 4 KOH(s) + O₂(g) --[KNO₃, heat]--> 2 K₂MnO₄(s) + 2 H₂O(g)

2. Disproportionation of A in acid:
3 MnO₄²⁻(aq) + 4 H⁺(aq) → 2 MnO₄⁻(aq) + MnO₂(s) + 2 H₂O(l)

3. Alkaline KMnO₄ (B) + KI:
2 MnO₄⁻(aq) + H₂O(l) + I⁻(aq) → 2 MnO₂(s) + 2 OH⁻(aq) + IO₃⁻(aq) [C = KIO₃]

4. Acidified KMnO₄ (B) + KI:
2 MnO₄⁻(aq) + 16 H⁺(aq) + 10 I⁻(aq) → 2 Mn²⁺(aq) + 8 H₂O(l) + 5 I₂(s) [D = I₂]

(B) Lanthanoid contraction is the progressive decrease in atomic and ionic radii across the lanthanoid series from La to Lu due to poor shielding of nuclear charge by 4f electrons.

(OR)

(b) (A) Filling of 4f orbitals prior to 5d elements causes lanthanoid contraction, which counteracts normal group size expansion, making Zr (160 pm) and Hf (159 pm) identical in radius.
(B) The energy gap between (n-1)d and ns orbitals is small, allowing electrons from both subshells to participate in bonding.
(C) Iron has 4 unpaired d-electrons contributing to strong interatomic metallic bonding.
(D) Addition of OH⁻ shifts the dichromate-chromate equilibrium to form yellow chromate ions:
Cr₂O₇²⁻(aq) [orange] + 2 OH⁻(aq) ⇌ 2 CrO₄²⁻(aq) [yellow] + H₂O(l)
