Chapter: Electrochemistry

33. (a) (i) Calculate the electrode potential of a half-cell for zinc electrode dipping in 0·01 M ZnSO₄ solution at 25°C. Given : E°(Zn²⁺/Zn) = − 0·76 V, [log 10 = 1] [2 Marks]
(ii) Write anode, cathode and overall reaction involved in dry cell. [2 Marks]
(iii) Equilibrium constant (Kc) is related to E°cell, but not to Ecell. Why ? [1 Mark]
OR
(b) (i) The conductivity of 0·001 M solution of acetic acid is 3·905 × 10⁻⁵ S cm⁻¹. Calculate its molar conductivity and degree of dissociation (α). Given : λ°(CH₃COO⁻) = 40·9 S cm² mol⁻¹, λ°(H⁺) = 349·6 S cm² mol⁻¹. [3 Marks]
(ii) Give reasons for the following : [2 Marks]
1. Why does a mercury cell deliver a constant voltage for its entire life ?
2. Why is it necessary to use salt bridge in a galvanic cell ?

## Answer

(a) (i) Electrode potential calculation:
E(Zn²⁺/Zn) = E°(Zn²⁺/Zn) - (0.0591 / 2) log (1 / [Zn²⁺])
E = -0.76 - 0.02955 × log (1 / 0.01) = -0.76 - 0.02955 × log (10²) = -0.76 - 0.0591 = -0.8191 V (or -0.82 V).
(ii) Dry cell reactions:
Anode: Zn(s) → Zn²⁺(aq) + 2 e⁻
Cathode: 2 MnO₂(s) + 2 NH₄⁺(aq) + 2 e⁻ → Mn₂O₃(s) + 2 NH₃(g) + H₂O(l)
Overall Reaction: Zn(s) + 2 MnO₂(s) + 2 NH₄⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Mn₂O₃(s) + 2 NH₃(g) + H₂O(l)
(iii) At equilibrium, net reaction stops and Ecell = 0; hence Ecell varies with concentration until equilibrium is reached. Standard cell potential E°cell is a constant thermodynamic value directly linked to Kc by E°cell = (RT / nF) ln Kc.
OR
(b) (i) Molar conductivity Λm = (κ × 1000) / c = (3.905 × 10⁻⁵ × 1000) / 0.001 = 39.05 S cm² mol⁻¹.
Limiting molar conductivity Λ°m = λ°(CH₃COO⁻) + λ°(H⁺) = 40.9 + 349.6 = 390.5 S cm² mol⁻¹.
Degree of dissociation α = Λm / Λ°m = 39.05 / 390.5 = 0.10 (or 10%).
(ii) 1. The overall cell reaction in a mercury cell (Zn(amalgam) + HgO(s) → ZnO(s) + Hg(l)) contains no ions in solution whose concentration changes during service.
2. A salt bridge completes the internal circuit by allowing ion migration and prevents accumulation of positive or negative charges in half-cells, maintaining electrical neutrality.
