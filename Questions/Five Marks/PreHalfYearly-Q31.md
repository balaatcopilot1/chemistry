Chapter: Chemical Kinetics

31. A. i) Calculate the overall order of the reaction which has the following rate expression:
Rate = k [A]¹/² [B]³/²
ii) What is the effect of temperature on rate of the reaction?
iii) A first order reaction takes 77.78 minutes for 50% completion. Calculate the time required for 30% completion of the reaction. (log 10 = 1, log 7 = 0.8450)
OR
B. i) For a first order reaction show that time required for 99% completion is twice the time required for 90% of the reaction.
ii) Observe the graph in diagram below and answer the following questions:
![Diagram](Questions/images/PreHalfYearly-Q31.png)
The graph shows a first-order kinetics plot of ln[R] versus time.
i) Identify the order.
ii) If slope is equal to -2.0 × 10⁻⁶ sec⁻¹, what will be the value of rate constant?

## Answer

A. (i) Overall Order = 1/2 + 3/2 = 4/2 = 2 (Second order).

(ii) Increasing temperature increases reaction rate significantly. Generally, for a 10 °C rise in temperature, the rate constant approximately doubles due to a larger fraction of molecules possessing energy ≥ activation energy.

(iii) Given: t₁/₂ = 77.78 min.
Step 1: k = 0.693 / t₁/₂ = 0.693 / 77.78 = 0.00891 min⁻¹ (or 2.303 × log 2 / 77.78).
Step 2: For 30% completion, remaining reactant = 100 - 30 = 70%.
t₃₀% = (2.303 / k) log (100 / 70) = (2.303 / 0.00891) × (log 10 - log 7)
t₃₀% = 258.47 × (1.0000 - 0.8450) = 258.47 × 0.1550 = 40.06 min.

OR

B. (i) Proof:
For 90% completion ([R]t = 10% of [R]₀):
t₉₀% = (2.303 / k) log (100 / 10) = (2.303 / k) log 10 = 2.303 / k

For 99% completion ([R]t = 1% of [R]₀):
t₉₉% = (2.303 / k) log (100 / 1) = (2.303 / k) log 10² = 2 × (2.303 / k)
Therefore, t₉₉% = 2 × t₉₀%.

(ii) Graph Analysis:
1. Order = First order (linear plot of ln[R] vs time).
2. Slope = -k ⇒ -k = -2.0 × 10⁻⁶ s⁻¹ ⇒ k = 2.0 × 10⁻⁶ s⁻¹.
