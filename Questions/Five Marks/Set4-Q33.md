Chapter: Electrochemistry

33. (a) (i) Calculate E°cell for the following reaction which is at equilibrium :
Cu (s) + 2Ag⁺(aq) ⇌ Cu²⁺(aq) + 2Ag (s)
Equilibrium constant (Kc) for the cell is 10¹⁵. [Given : log 10 = 1]
(ii) Write anode, cathode and overall reaction of lead storage battery when it is in use.
(iii) How much electricity is required in coulombs for the oxidation of 1 mol of FeO to Fe₂O₃ ?
OR
(b) (i) Conductivity of 0.0024 M acetic acid is 7.2 × 10⁻⁵ S cm⁻¹. If Λ°m for acetic acid is 390.5 S cm² mol⁻¹, then calculate the degree of dissociation (α) of acetic acid.
(ii) Calculate the cell potential for the following half cell reaction at 25 ºC :
Ag⁺(aq) + 1 e⁻ → Ag (s)
Given that : [Ag⁺] = 0.01 M and E° (Ag⁺/Ag) = +0.80 V [Given : log 10 = 1]
(iii) Write the name of the electrolyte used in :
1. Dry cell
2. Fuel cell (H₂ – O₂)

## Answer

(a) (i) Calculation of E°cell:
Formula: E°cell = (0.0591 / n) log Kc (n = 2)
Substitution: E°cell = (0.0591 / 2) log(10¹⁵) = 0.02955 × 15 = 0.443 V.

(ii) Lead Storage Battery Discharging Reactions:
Anode: Pb(s) + SO₄²⁻(aq) → PbSO₄(s) + 2 e⁻
Cathode: PbO₂(s) + SO₄²⁻(aq) + 4 H⁺(aq) + 2 e⁻ → PbSO₄(s) + 2 H₂O(l)
Overall Reaction: Pb(s) + PbO₂(s) + 2 H₂SO₄(aq) → 2 PbSO₄(s) + 2 H₂O(l)

(iii) Electricity Required:
Oxidation reaction: 2 FeO + H₂O → Fe₂O₃ + 2 H⁺ + 2 e⁻ (1 mole FeO releases 1 mole e⁻).
Q = 1 F = 96,500 C.

OR

(b) (i) Molar conductivity Λm = (κ × 1000) / c = (7.2 × 10⁻⁵ × 1000) / 0.0024 = 30 S cm² mol⁻¹.
Degree of dissociation α = Λm / Λ°m = 30 / 390.5 = 0.0768 (or 7.68%).

(ii) Half-cell potential by Nernst equation:
E = E° - (0.0591 / 1) log(1 / [Ag⁺])
E = +0.80 - 0.0591 × log(1 / 0.01) = 0.80 - 0.0591 × log(10²) = 0.80 - 0.1182 = 0.6818 V (or ~0.68 V).

(iii) 1. Dry cell: Moist paste of NH₄Cl and ZnCl₂.
2. Fuel cell: Concentrated aqueous KOH solution.
