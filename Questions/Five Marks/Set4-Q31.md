Chapter: The d- and f-Block Elements

31. (a) (i) Account for the following :
1. MnO is basic while Mn₂O₇ is acidic.
2. Iron has higher enthalpy of atomization than that of copper.
3. Mn³⁺ is a stronger oxidising agent than Cr³⁺.
(ii) How do you prepare potassium dichromate from sodium chromate ? Write balanced chemical equation for each step.
OR
(b) Answer the following questions :
1. Why is chemistry of actinoids more complicated as compared to lanthanoids ?
2. E° (M²⁺/M) values are not regular for first row transition elements (3d series). Why ?
3. Identify the following :
(1) Oxoanion of chromium which is stable in acidic medium.
(2) The lanthanoid element that exhibits +4 oxidation state.
4. Write the products obtained on heating KMnO₄.
5. Predict which of the following ions will be coloured in aqueous solution : Cu⁺, Sc³⁺, Fe²⁺, Ti³⁺, Zn²⁺.

## Answer

(a) (i) 1. In MnO, Mn is in a low +2 oxidation state with predominant ionic character, making it basic. In Mn₂O₇, Mn is in a high +7 oxidation state where high polarizing power leads to covalent character, making it acidic.
2. Iron (3d⁶ 4s²) has 4 unpaired electrons contributing to strong interatomic metallic bonding, whereas copper (3d¹⁰ 4s¹) has filled d-orbitals with fewer available unpaired electrons for bonding.
3. Reduction of Mn³⁺ (3d⁴) to Mn²⁺ yields a highly stable half-filled 3d⁵ configuration, while Cr³⁺ (3d³) already possesses a stable half-filled t₂g³ configuration.

(ii) Preparation of K₂Cr₂O₇ from Na₂CrO₄:
Step 1: Acidification of sodium chromate to sodium dichromate:
2 Na₂CrO₄(aq) + H₂SO₄(aq) → Na₂Cr₂O⇇(aq) + Na₂SO₄(aq) + H₂O(l)
Step 2: Conversion to potassium dichromate by potassium chloride:
Na₂Cr₂O⇇(aq) + 2 KCl(aq) → K₂Cr₂O⇇(s)↓ + 2 NaCl(aq)

OR

(b) 1. Actinoids show a wide variety of oxidation states (+3 to +7) because 5f, 6d, and 7s levels are close in energy, and many actinoids are radioactive.
2. Irregular E° (M²⁺/M) values arise from irregular variations in sublimation enthalpies and ionization energies (ΔiH₁ + ΔiH₂), and hydration energies across the series.
3. (1) Dichromate ion (Cr₂O₇²⁻)
(2) Cerium (Ce)
4. Thermal decomposition of KMnO₄:
2 KMnO₄(s) --[heat]--> K₂MnO₄(s) + MnO₂(s) + O₂(g)↑
5. Coloured ions have incompletely filled d-orbitals for d-d transitions: Fe²⁺ (3d⁶) and Ti³⁺ (3d¹) are coloured; Cu⁺ (3d¹⁰), Sc³⁺ (3d⁰), and Zn²⁺ (3d¹⁰) are colourless.

*Source: NCERT, The d- and f-Block Elements*
