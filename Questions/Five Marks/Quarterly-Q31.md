Chapter: Electrochemistry

31. (a) Define the following terms:
i) Limiting molar conductivity
ii) State one advantage of H₂-O₂ fuel cell over ordinary cell.
iii) State Kohlrausch law of independent migration of ions.
iv) Resistance of a conductivity cell filled with 0.1 mol L⁻¹ KCl solution is 100 ohm. The resistance of the same cell when filled with 0.02 mol L⁻¹ KCl solution is 520 ohm. Calculate the conductivity and molar conductivity of 0.02 mol L⁻¹ KCl solution. The conductivity of 0.1 mol L⁻¹ KCl solution is 1.29 × 10⁻² ohm⁻¹ cm⁻¹.
(OR)
(b) The electrochemical cell given inside converts the chemical energy released during the redox reaction. Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) -> Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s) to electrical energy. It gives an electrical potential of 1.1 V when concentration Zn²⁺ and Cu²⁺ ions is unity. State the direction of flow of current and also specify whether zinc and copper are deposited or dissolved at their respective electrodes when:
(i) an external opposite potential of less than 1.1 V is applied
(ii) an external potential of 1.1 V is applied.
(iii) an external potential of greater than 1.1 V is applied.
iv) Write the cell reaction and calculate the e.m.f. of the following cell at 298 K:
Sn(s) | Sn²⁺ (0.004 M) || H⁺ (0.020 M) | H₂(g) (1 bar) | Pt(s). (Given :: E°Sn²⁺/Sn = -0.14V)

## Answer

(a) (i) Limiting Molar Conductivity (Λm°):
The molar conductivity of an electrolyte solution when its concentration approaches zero (at infinite dilution).

(ii) Advantage of H₂-O₂ Fuel Cell:
High efficiency (~70% compared to ~40% for thermal plants) and zero pollution since water vapor is the only byproduct.

(iii) Kohlrausch Law:
The limiting molar conductivity of an electrolyte can be represented as the sum of the individual ionic conductivities of the cation and anion, each multiplied by the number of ions present in one formula unit.

(iv) Calculation:
- Cell Constant, G* = κ × R = (1.29 × 10⁻² S cm⁻¹) × 100 Ω = 1.29 cm⁻¹
- Conductivity of 0.02 M KCl, κ = G* / R = 1.29 cm⁻¹ / 520 Ω = 2.48 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹
- Molar Conductivity, Λm = (κ × 1000) / c = (2.48 × 10⁻³ × 1000) / 0.02 = 124 S cm² mol⁻¹

Answer (a.iv): κ = 2.48 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹; Λm = 124 S cm² mol⁻¹

(OR)

(b) (i) Eext < 1.1 V: Current flows from Cu to Zn (electrons from Zn to Cu). Zinc dissolves at anode and copper deposits at cathode.
(ii) Eext = 1.1 V: No current flows, no chemical reaction occurs, no dissolution or deposition.
(iii) Eext > 1.1 V: Reaction reverses (cell functions as electrolytic cell). Current flows from Zn to Cu. Copper dissolves and zinc deposits on zinc electrode.

(iv) EMF Calculation:
Overall Cell Reaction: Sn(s) + 2 H⁺(aq) → Sn²⁺(aq) + H₂(g)
E°cell = E°cathode - E°anode = 0.00 - (-0.14) = +0.14 V

Nernst Equation (n = 2):
Ecell = E°cell - (0.0591 / 2) log ([Sn²⁺] / [H⁺]²)
Ecell = 0.14 - 0.02955 log (0.004 / (0.020)²)
Ecell = 0.14 - 0.02955 log (0.004 / 0.0004) = 0.14 - 0.02955 log (10)
Ecell = 0.14 - 0.02955 = 0.11045 V ≈ 0.11 V

Answer (b.iv): Ecell = 0.11 V
