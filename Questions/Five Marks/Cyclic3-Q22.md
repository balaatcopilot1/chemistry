Chapter: Electrochemistry

22. i) Calculate E°cell for the following reaction at 298 K :
2Cr(s) + 3Fe²⁺(0.01M) → 2Cr³⁺(0.01M) + 3Fe(s)  (Given : Ecell = 0.261 V, log 10 = 1)
ii) What is the product of hydrolysis of dilute sulphuric acid using Pt electrode?
iii) A rusted piece of iron undergoes electrochemical reaction. Write the reaction taking place at the cathode.

## Answer

(i) Step 1: Write the Nernst Equation for the cell:
Reaction: 2 Cr(s) + 3 Fe²⁺(aq) → 2 Cr³⁺(aq) + 3 Fe(s)
Number of electrons transferred, n = 6

Nernst Equation:
Ecell = E°cell - (0.0591 / n) log ([Cr³⁺]² / [Fe²⁺]³)

Step 2: Substitute given values:
Ecell = 0.261 V
[Cr³⁺] = 0.01 M = 10⁻² M
[Fe²⁺] = 0.01 M = 10⁻² M

log ([Cr³⁺]² / [Fe²⁺]³) = log ((10⁻²)² / (10⁻²)³) = log (10⁻⁴ / 10⁻⁶) = log (10²) = 2

0.261 = E°cell - (0.0591 / 6) × 2
0.261 = E°cell - 0.0197
E°cell = 0.261 + 0.0197 = 0.2807 V (or ~ 0.281 V)

(ii) Electrolysis of dilute H₂SO₄ using Pt electrode:
- At Anode: Water is oxidized to liberate Oxygen gas (O₂).
  2 H₂O(l) → O₂(g) + 4 H⁺(aq) + 4 e⁻
- At Cathode: H⁺ ions are reduced to liberate Hydrogen gas (H₂).
  2 H⁺(aq) + 2 e⁻ → H₂(g)
Products: Hydrogen gas (H₂) at cathode and Oxygen gas (O₂) at anode.

(iii) Cathodic Reaction during Rusting of Iron:
At the cathodic area, oxygen in the presence of H⁺ ions (from dissolved CO₂/H₂O) is reduced to water.

Cathode Equation:
O₂(g) + 4 H⁺(aq) + 4 e⁻ → 2 H₂O(l)
