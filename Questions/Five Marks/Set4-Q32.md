Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

32. (a) (i) How will you bring about the following conversions :
1. Bromobenzene to 1-phenylethanol
2. Benzene to m-nitroacetophenone
(ii) An organic compound with the molecular formula C₈H₈O forms 2,4-DNP derivative, reduces Tollens’ reagent and undergoes Cannizzaro reaction. On vigorous oxidation with acidic or alkaline KMnO₄ it gives Benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid. Identify the compound and write the products when it undergoes Cannizzaro reaction.
(iii) Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their acid strength :
C₆H₅COOH, O₂N – CH₂ – COOH, CF₃ – COOH, HCOOH
OR
(b) (i) Write the products formed when benzaldehyde reacts with the following reagents :
1. 2,4-Dinitrophenylhydrazine
2. Acetophenone in the presence of dilute NaOH followed by heating
(ii) Give reasons for the following :
1. Benzoic acid does not undergo Friedel-Crafts reaction.
2. Carboxylic acids have higher boiling point than alcohols of comparable molecular mass.
(iii) Write simple chemical test to distinguish between propanal and propanone.

## Answer

(a) (i) 1. Convert bromobenzene to Grignard reagent (C₆H₅MgBr) with Mg/dry ether, react with ethanal (CH₃CHO), and hydrolyze with H₃O⁺ to yield 1-phenylethanol.
2. Acetylate benzene with CH₃COCl/anhydrous AlCl₃ to get acetophenone, then nitrate with conc. HNO₃ + conc. H₂SO₄ to obtain m-nitroacetophenone.

(ii) Compound Identification:
Formula C₈H₈O reducing Tollens' reagent and undergoing Cannizzaro reaction indicates an aromatic aldehyde lacking α-hydrogens. Oxidation to benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid confirms an ortho-substituted benzaldehyde derivative:
Compound = 2-Methylbenzaldehyde (o-tolualdehyde).
Cannizzaro products with conc. KOH:
2 (2-CH₃–C₆H₄–CHO) + KOH → 2-CH₃–C₆H₄–COOK (Potassium 2-methylbenzoate) + 2-CH₃–C₆H₄–CH₂OH (2-Methylbenzyl alcohol).

(iii) Increasing Acid Strength:
C₆H₅COOH < HCOOH < O₂N–CH₂–COOH < CF₃–COOH
(Strong electron-withdrawing groups like -NO₂ and -CF₃ increase acid strength by inductive effect).

OR

(b) (i) 1. Forms Benzaldehyde 2,4-dinitrophenylhydrazone (yellow-orange ppt).
2. Cross-aldol condensation gives 1,3-diphenylprop-2-en-1-one (benzalacetophenone).

(ii) 1. Carboxylic group (-COOH) is strongly deactivating and deactivates the ring; also, Lewis acid catalyst AlCl₃ forms a salt complex with -COOH.
2. Carboxylic acids form stable hydrogen-bonded dimers in gas and liquid phases.

(iii) Tollens' Test: Propanal reduces Tollens' reagent to give a bright silver mirror, whereas propanone does not.
