Chapter: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

33. (a) i) An organic compound 'A' having molecular formula C₄H₈O gives orange-red precipitate with 2,4-DNP reagent. It does not reduce Tollen's reagent but gives yellow precipitate of iodoform on heating with NaOI. Compound A on reduction with NaBH₄ gives compound 'B', which undergoes dehydration reaction on heating with conc. H₂SO₄ to form Compound C. On Ozonolysis gives two molecules of ethanal. Identify the compounds A, B and C and write their structures and their reactions.
(ii) Reason out for the following: Oxidation of pentanal is easier than Pentan-2-one. Why? And write the products for the same.
(OR)
(b) Explain the following: (i) Decarboxylation (ii) Etard reaction (iii) HVZ Reaction
(c) Convert the following: (i) Phthalic acid to Phthalimide (ii) ethanal to but-2-enal.

## Answer

(a) (i) Identification:
- Compound A (C₄H₈O) gives 2,4-DNP test, negative Tollen's, and positive iodoform test → A is Butan-2-one (CH₃COCH₂CH₃).
- Reduction of A with NaBH₄ gives B → B is Butan-2-ol [CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CH₃].
- Acid dehydration of B gives C → C is But-2-ene (CH₃CH=CHCH₃).
- Ozonolysis of C (But-2-ene) yields 2 molecules of ethanal (CH₃CHO).

Structures:
A = CH₃–CO–CH₂–CH₃ (Butan-2-one)
B = CH₃–CH(OH)–CH₂–CH₃ (Butan-2-ol)
C = CH₃–CH=CH–CH₃ (But-2-ene)

Reactions:
1. Reduction: CH₃COCH₂CH₃ + NaBH₄ → CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CH₃
2. Dehydration: CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CH₃ --[conc H₂SO₄, heat]--> CH₃CH=CHCH₃ + H₂O
3. Ozonolysis: CH₃CH=CHCH₃ + O₃ --[Zn/H₂O]--> 2 CH₃CHO

(ii) Pentanal (aldehyde) has an easily oxidizable C–H bond on the carbonyl carbon, permitting mild oxidation to pentanoic acid without C–C bond cleavage. Pentan-2-one (ketone) lacks a C–H bond on the carbonyl carbon and requires strong oxidation conditions involving C–C bond cleavage to yield ethanoic and propanoic acids.

(OR)

(b) Named Reactions:
(i) Decarboxylation: Carboxylic acid sodium salts heated with soda lime (NaOH + CaO) lose CO₂ to form alkanes with one less carbon.
RCOONa + NaOH --[CaO, heat]--> R–H + Na₂CO₃
(ii) Etard Reaction: Toluene oxidized with chromyl chloride (CrO₂Cl₂) in CS₂ forms a chromium complex, which on hydrolysis yields benzaldehyde.
C₆H₅CH₃ + 2 CrO₂Cl₂ --[CS₂; H₃O⁺]--> C₆H₅CHO + 2 Cr(OH)Cl₂
(iii) HVZ Reaction: Carboxylic acids with α-hydrogens are brominated at the α-position using Br₂/red P followed by hydrolysis.
CH₃COOH + Br₂ --[red P / H₂O]--> ClCH₂COOH + HBr

(c) Conversions:
(i) Phthalic Acid to Phthalimide: Heat phthalic acid with ammonia to form ammonium phthalate, which on strong heating forms phthalimide.
C₆H₄(COOH)₂ + 2 NH₃ --[heat, -2H₂O]--> C₆H₄(CONH₂)₂ --[strong heat, -NH₃]--> C₆H₄(CO)₂NH
(ii) Ethanal to But-2-enal: Aldol condensation of ethanal with dilute NaOH followed by heating.
2 CH₃CHO --[dil NaOH]--> CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CHO --[heat, -H₂O]--> CH₃CH=CHCHO
