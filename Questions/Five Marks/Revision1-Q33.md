Chapter: Amines

33. (a) Account for the following.
(A) Why Aniline cannot be prepared by ammonolysis of chlorobenzene under normal conditions?
(B) N–ethylethanamine boils at 329.3 K and butanamine boils at 350.8 K although both are isomeric in nature. Explain
(C) Why acylation of aniline carried out in the presence of pyridine?
(D) Why aniline does not react with methyl chloride in presence of anhydrous AlCl₃?
(OR)
(b) Gopikhan was given two test tubes. One of the test tubes contained ethyl amine and the Other contained aniline. To distinguish between the two compounds, He adds a reagent X to both the test tubes. He observes that in only one of the test tubes a yellow dye is formed.
(A) Identify the reagent X.
(B) Which of the two compounds forms the yellow dye?
(C) Draw the structure of the yellow dye formed.
(D) Explain the above test with suitable reactions.

## Answer

(a) (A) Chlorobenzene has partial double bond character in the C–Cl bond due to resonance delocalization of chlorine's lone pair, making it difficult to cleave under normal conditions.
(B) Butanamine (1° amine) has two N–H hydrogens capable of extensive intermolecular hydrogen bonding. N-ethylethanamine (2° amine) has only one N–H hydrogen, resulting in weaker hydrogen bonding and a lower boiling point.
(C) Pyridine is a stronger base than aniline and neutralizes the HCl byproduct formed during acylation, shifting the reaction equilibrium forward.
(D) Aniline forms a complex salt with the Lewis acid catalyst AlCl₃ (–NH₂⁺–AlCl₃⁻), which strongly deactivates the aromatic ring against Friedel-Crafts alkylation.

(OR)

(b) (A) Reagent X: Nitrous acid (NaNO₂ + HCl at 273–278 K) followed by alkaline phenol (Azo Dye Test).
(B) Aniline forms the yellow dye.
(C) Structure of Yellow Dye (p-hydroxyazobenzene): C₆H₅–N=N–C₆H₄–OH
(D) Diazotization of aniline yields benzenediazonium chloride, which couples with phenol to form yellow-orange p-hydroxyazobenzene:
C₆H₅NH₂ + NaNO₂ + 2 HCl --[273-278 K]--> C₆H₅N₂⁺Cl⁻ + NaCl + 2 H₂O
C₆H₅N₂⁺Cl⁻ + C₆H₅OH + NaOH → C₆H₅–N=N–C₆H₄–OH [yellow dye] + NaCl + H₂O
